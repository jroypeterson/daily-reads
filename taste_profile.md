## Preference Summary

### Topics I Gravitate Toward
- **Investing — value & quality, capital allocation.** Howard Marks-style risk/temperament, intrinsic value, second-level/contrarian thinking; the compounder & serial-acquirer playbook (Constellation, Danaher) judged on RoIC + organic growth, incentives, and management quality.
- **Credit & leveraged finance.** The deepest single study area — high-yield/corporate debt, covenants as strategy signals, CDS/basis, default & recovery math. Credit structure interests me as much as the equity story.
- **Short selling, market microstructure, hedge-fund/PE craft.** Loss/winner management, valuation shorts, HFT, the mechanics of the buy side.
- **Healthcare across the stack.** Health IT/EHR and clinical workflow, medtech device innovation (Biodesign), genomics, healthcare political economy & regulation, and biotech business narratives (Theranos, drug-deal sagas). This is my professional edge — HC Services, MedTech, Biopharma, Health IT.
- **Macro & top-down.** Currencies, cycles, central banking, endowment-style portfolio construction.
- **VC / angel / company-building.** Fund mechanics, term sheets, founder ops, product strategy.
- **Product & design craft.** Iterative building, usability, PM thinking, org/operating systems (High Output Management).
- **Epistemic hygiene & quant tooling.** Statistical skepticism, probabilistic risk measurement, algorithms, R, cybersecurity risk.
- **Epic sci-fi & fantasy.** A genuine second track — Sanderson's Cosmere above all, plus hard SF (Asimov, Heinlein, Stephenson) and epic fantasy (Weeks, Rothfuss, Grossman, Martin). Drawn to morality-of-one's-own-heart, identity-as-narrative, and power/hierarchy themes.
- **Heterodox economics & social conscience.** Critiques of shareholder-value orthodoxy and free-market absolutism; mass incarceration & race.

### Qualities I Value
- **Frameworks, checklists, formulas, and crisp categorizations** I can operationalize — anything clean enough to turn into a spaced-repetition (Anki) card lands hardest.
- **Process over outcome.** A well-reasoned wrong call beats a lucky right one; show the reasoning, not just the conclusion.
- **The contrarian / under-followed angle** — the things "other investors can't or won't do"; complexity and being misunderstood are features, not bugs.
- **Depth and primary thinking** over hot-takes; long-form that rewards a careful read.
- **Synthesis across mental models** — content that stress-tests one framework against another (e.g. Porter vs. Greenwald) rather than preaching a single lens.
- **Builder's eye** — pieces that expose a real operating problem and a fixable mechanism.
- **High prose quality** — structured, vivid, no filler.

### What I Tend to Skip
_(Inferred from what is absent in a large, deliberate library — lower confidence than the above.)_
- Pure punditry / news-of-the-day with no underlying model or durable takeaway.
- Celebrity, lifestyle, and engagement-bait clickbait.
- Surface-level "X tips" listicles that don't expose mechanism or transfer to a framework.
- Promotional/marketing content dressed as analysis.

### Source & Style Affinities
- **Long-form books** are the backbone; **curated buy-side Twitter/X threads** are a deliberate reps channel (10kdiver, Brett Caughran/FundamentEdge, LongShortHC, SecretCFO, ShaneParrish, the O'Shaughnessys, MnkeDaniel/Nick Sleep).
- **Investor letters & deep-dive newsletters** (Scott Letters, The Transcript, Value Investor Insight, A Letter a Day, Substack deep dives like "Studying Serial Acquirers").
- **Practitioner/operator voices** over academic abstraction — though formal rigor (CFA-level, CLRS, Graham & Dodd) is welcome when it earns its keep.
- **Trusted authors:** Howard Marks, Brandon Sanderson, William Green, Michael Lewis, Robert Wachter, Ha-Joon Chang.
- Reads to **act and to remember**: annotates deepest-study material with recall prompts and Anki flags.
