Intuitive Surgical: The Long Road to Everyday Surgery

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-10-28 · ⏱ 18 min read

MBI Deep Dives traces Intuitive Surgical's 30-year arc from a DoD-funded telepresence prototype to a 10,000-procedures-per-day platform, examining how the absorption of rival Computer Motion consolidated the foundational patent estate and enabled profitability. 5 payers suing over 2027 Medicare Advantage star ratings

Becker's Payer Issues · 2026-10-28 · ⏱ 3 min read

At least five health insurers—CVS/Aetna, SCAN, Centene, Healthfirst, and Alignment Healthcare—have sued CMS over their 2027 Medicare Advantage star ratings, alleging the agency applied quality measures never formally adopted through notice-and-comment rulemaking. The combined financial exposure exceeds $3 billion in foregone bonus payments and rebate retention, with CVS alone claiming over $2 billion in 2028 impact. The litigation follows a multi-year pattern of payer-CMS disputes over MA rating methodology, including a 2026 Georgia federal court ruling that found CMS lacked authority for 20 measures in Clover Health's rating.

💡 With CVS/Aetna, Centene, and Alignment Healthcare all facing material bonus payment shortfalls—quantified in the billions—this litigation wave represents a concrete, near-term earnings risk event for managed care investors heading into 2027 plan year budgeting cycles.
