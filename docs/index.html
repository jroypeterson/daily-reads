Breaking: FDA Approves Moderna's mRNA Flu Vaccine

STAT News · 2026-08-08 · ⏱ 4 min read

The FDA approved Moderna's mFlusiva (mRNA-1010), the first mRNA-based seasonal influenza vaccine, for adults 50 and older — marking a significant platform validation for Moderna's mRNA technology beyond COVID. Full approval was granted for ages 50-64 based on a 40,000-person Phase 3 trial demonstrating ~27% superior efficacy versus standard flu shots, while accelerated approval was extended to adults 65+ pending a confirmatory post-market study. The clearance is notable given active political headwinds from HHS Secretary RFK Jr.'s opposition to mRNA vaccines, representing a meaningful test of FDA's independence under the current administration.

💡 This approval opens a large, recurring annual flu vaccine market for Moderna, validates the mRNA platform's commercial and regulatory viability beyond COVID, and sets a precedent for how the FDA navigates political pressure on mRNA-based therapeutics — all of which carry direct implications for MRNA's pipeline valuation.

🌀 Slot 4

The AI Boom Could Boost Epic's Position, Execs Say — Here's Where AI Startups Can Still Win

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-08-08 · ⏱ 6 min read

A Redesign Health survey of 112 senior health system executives reveals that 71% describe their organizations as 'Epic-first,' with 80% expecting that bias to intensify over three to five years, as Epic aggressively integrates ambient AI scribing (Art), revenue cycle AI (Penny), and patient AI (Emmie) into its EHR platform. Despite 91% of executives expressing high confidence in Epic's AI roadmap, 64% remain open to purchasing from net-new startups — particularly in areas requiring outside-healthcare data exchange, deep clinical specialization (imaging, smart intake), or faster time-to-value than Epic delivers. The survey identifies five durable startup niches: clinician-facing AI, imaging AI, patient AI assistants, discharge/care transitions, and revenue cycle AI.

💡 For investors evaluating health software and digital health SaaS, this provides a data-backed framework for distinguishing which AI-in-healthcare categories face Epic consolidation risk versus which have structural moats — directly actionable for portfolio construction and competitive diligence.
