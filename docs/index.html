Centene Boosts Guidance Amid $1.1B Q2 Profit, ACA Membership Drop

Becker's Payer Issues / Fierce Healthcare · 2026-07-28 · ⏱ 2 min read

Centene reported a $1.1B Q2 2026 profit versus a $253M loss a year prior, with total revenue of $53.6B (up ~10% YoY) and raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance to above $4.80. ACA marketplace membership dropped sharply to 3.5M from 5.9M a year ago, while Medicaid and Medicare membership also declined, though Medicare PDP participation rose by ~1M. The health benefits ratio improved significantly to 89.6% from 93%, driving the profitability swing.

💡 CNC's dramatic margin recovery and guidance raise — alongside notable membership mix shifts away from ACA and Medicaid — serves as a bellwether for managed care sector health under ongoing Medicaid reform and ACA enrollment pressure from the current policy regime.
