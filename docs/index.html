Novo Sues Lilly Over 'False' GLP-1 Ads

BioPharma Dive · 2026-07-22 · ⏱ 3 min read

Novo Nordisk has filed suit in New Jersey federal court against Eli Lilly, alleging that Lilly's consumer ads for Zepbound and Mounjaro deliberately compare the highest doses of Lilly's drugs against older, lower doses of Wegovy and Ozempic — omitting Novo's newly approved 7.2mg Wegovy dose. Lilly defends its campaign as grounded in the Surmount-5 head-to-head trial, the only randomized comparison of the two drugs in weight management, while Novo argues that trial's results are now misleading given the new higher dose approval. The dispute reflects intensifying market share competition: Zepbound generated $4B in Q1 2026 sales vs. $2.7B for Wegovy.

💡 The legal and marketing battle between NVO and LLY over GLP-1 market share has direct implications for pricing power, revenue trajectories, and competitive positioning in pharma's highest-revenue franchise — with a court injunction or corrective advertising campaign potentially reshaping consumer perception heading into obesity pill launches.
