Scholar Rock SMA drug approved by FDA

BioPharma Dive · 2026-09-14 · ⏱ 4 min read

The FDA approved Scholar Rock's apitegromab (Isembyld) as the first muscle-targeted therapy for spinal muscular atrophy, cleared for use alongside existing SMN2-targeted treatments in adults and children 2+. The approval follows a prior rejection due to manufacturing facility issues, which Scholar Rock resolved by switching fill-finish sites. Analysts at Leerink Partners project peak annual sales of ~$2 billion, and the company also received a priority review voucher that can be sold for nine-figure sums.

💡 This is a binary catalyst resolved in SRRK's favor — the first approval for a novel mechanism in an established rare disease market — with a clear commercial launch underway, blockbuster revenue potential, and a monetizable PRV providing additional near-term upside.
