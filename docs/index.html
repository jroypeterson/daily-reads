From Supplier to Platform: China's Bid to Be the World's AI Stack

A Letter a Day (Substack) · 2026-09-13 · ⏱ 18 min read

The memo argues that China's embrace of open-weight frontier models (DeepSeek et al.) is not altruism or ideology but a calculated platform strategy: release models freely to win the global developer base, then pull silicon and infrastructure purchases onto the Chinese AI stack (Huawei Ascend, MindSpore). It traces the inversion of the historical US-open/China-closed paradigm and grounds the thesis in Jensen Huang's five-layer AI stack framework, showing that model optimization for a given chip architecture is the real lock-in mechanism. Recent developments—Saudi Arabia's national AI platform built on MiniMax, DeepSeek's newest model—are cited as early evidence the wedge is working.

💡 For investors long NVDA or US hyperscaler capex, this is a structural risk framework: if open-weight models become optimized for Huawei silicon and pull emerging-market AI infrastructure spend outside the US stack, it is a long-duration threat to NVDA's moat that earnings calls and near-term capex data will not reveal.

🌀 Slot 4

TransDigm Is Still In It for the Money

Scuttleblurb · 2026-09-13 · ⏱ 12 min read

Seven years after an initial writeup, Scuttleblurb revisits TransDigm to assess whether its core value-creation mechanics—sole-source proprietary parts, aftermarket pricing power, and an acquisition flywheel—have survived a pandemic, inflation shock, and CEO transition. The piece argues the model remains structurally intact: ~80% of revenue from sole-source parts, ~90% from proprietary IP, and 52.8% EBITDA margins with 13% organic growth in Q3 FY26. The key insight is that TransDigm's 'forgettable but essential' low-dollar components are precisely what insulates it from customer pushback—nobody halts aircraft production over a $500 hinge.

💡 For investors tracking serial acquirers with pricing power, this is a live stress-test of whether TDG's moat framework holds post-pandemic and post-inflation—with fresh Q3 earnings data making it immediately actionable for portfolio positioning.
