McKinsey: Physical AI — The hidden value pools leaders are missing

McKinsey · 2026-10-04 · ⏱ 6 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

McKinsey argues that 'physical AI' — AI embedded in physical systems like surgical robotics, manufacturing intelligence, and logistics automation — represents a set of value pools that corporate leaders are systematically underestimating relative to software-centric AI narratives. The piece maps where economic value from AI will actually accrue in the physical world, moving beyond the humanoid robot hype cycle. Healthcare robotics and diagnostics hardware are highlighted as particularly underappreciated growth vectors.
