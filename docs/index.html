🤖 Slot 3

License to Chase the AI Frontier

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-12 · ⏱ 8 min read

MBI Deep Dives analyzes the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and observes that virtually all top-ranked frontier AI model developers are founder-led companies, arguing this is not coincidental but structural: founders have voting control, cultural capital, and a 'relevance' motivation that lets them absorb uncertain, capital-intensive R&D that manager-run companies (Apple, Microsoft, Amazon) cannot credibly sustain. The piece updates the author's prior skepticism of frontier lab economics, now centering on proprietary data accumulation — illustrated by Meta giving away its coding model free in exchange for user data — as the likely source of durable competitive advantage. It concludes that investors face a genuine underwriting challenge in modeling the payoff curve across frontier tiers, which is why many prefer selling compute capacity to laggards over backing frontier leaders.

💡 Investors sizing positions in AI infrastructure or model providers need a framework for distinguishing which companies have earned a credible 'license to spend' at the frontier — this piece offers exactly that, with implications for how to weight hyperscalers vs. pure-play frontier labs in a portfolio.
