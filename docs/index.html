🤖 Slot 3

OpenAI makes Health in ChatGPT widely available, moving deeper into consumer health

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-07-24 · ⏱ 5 min read

OpenAI has broadly launched Health in ChatGPT for U.S. users, enabling connection of medical records (via Epic, Oracle Health, One Medical, Function Health) and Apple Health wearable data through a B.Well partnership, transforming ChatGPT from a generic health Q&A tool into a personalized health data interface. The rollout introduces new model versions — GPT-5.5 Instant (free tier) and GPT-5.6 Sol (paid) — purpose-optimized for health reasoning, evaluated against HealthBench Professional benchmarks with input from 200+ physician advisors across 60 countries. Connected health data receives enhanced encryption and is explicitly excluded from model training, addressing a key adoption barrier.

💡 OpenAI entering consumer health data aggregation at scale — with direct EHR integrations already live — is a category-defining competitive threat to health IT platforms (Epic's patient engagement layer, HCAT, Veradigm) and a significant validation signal for the AI-in-healthcare infrastructure thesis that warrants immediate portfolio review.
