FDA approves Gilead's Hepcludex as first treatment for hepatitis delta virus

Multiple Healthcare Sources · 2026-05-27 · ⏱ 3 min read

The FDA granted accelerated approval to Gilead's Hepcludex (bulevirtide) as the first-ever treatment for chronic hepatitis delta virus in the US, affecting an estimated 40,000-80,000 Americans. The approval was based on Phase 3 data showing statistically significant virologic and biochemical response at 48 weeks, though the drug carries a boxed warning for severe hepatitis exacerbations after discontinuation.

💡 This represents Gilead's entry into a previously untreatable rare disease market with no competition, potentially generating meaningful revenue while addressing a severe liver condition with high mortality risk.
