FDA Approves Merck's LIPFENDRA (enlicitide) — First Oral PCSK9 Inhibitor

STAT News · 2026-07-16 · ⏱ 3 min read

The FDA approved Merck's LIPFENDRA (enlicitide) as the first oral PCSK9 inhibitor, demonstrating 56–59% placebo-adjusted LDL-C reduction in the CORALreef pivotal trials. The pill format represents a formulation breakthrough over the injectable incumbents (Amgen's Repatha, Sanofi/Regeneron's Praluent), with convenience advantages expected to significantly expand the addressable patient population. The approval sets up competitive read-throughs across cardiovascular pipelines and likely accelerates market share dynamics in the $15B+ PCSK9 class.

💡 A first-in-class oral PCSK9 approval is a blockbuster catalyst for MRK and a structural threat to injectable PCSK9 incumbents, with downstream implications for AstraZeneca, Novartis, and Alnylam cardiovascular franchises.
