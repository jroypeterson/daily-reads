FDA Approves First Oral PCSK9 Inhibitor to Lower LDL Cholesterol in Adults with High Cholesterol

FDA Press Releases · 2026-07-18 · ⏱ 3 min read

The FDA approved Merck's Lipfendra (enlicitide) on July 16, 2026 as the first oral PCSK9 inhibitor, administered as a once-daily tablet for adults with hypercholesterolemia including HeFH. Phase 3 trials across 3,207 patients showed placebo-adjusted LDL-C reductions of 56–59% at Week 24, comparable to existing injectable PCSK9 inhibitors like Repatha and Praluent. The drug received Priority Review and was approved as an adjunct to maximally tolerated statin therapy, with a cardiovascular outcomes trial still ongoing.

💡 Oral delivery removes the primary real-world adoption barrier that has constrained the injectable PCSK9 market, potentially unlocking a much larger patient population and directly threatening Amgen (AMGN) and Regeneron/Sanofi's franchise revenue while creating a significant new growth driver for Merck (MRK).

🤖 Slot 3

Cost versus value: Managing agentic AI system performance

McKinsey · 2026-07-18 · ⏱ 5 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

McKinsey's Q&A with Pay-i CEO David Tepper unpacks the hidden economics of agentic AI, revealing that roughly 60% of token costs stem from refinement and re-verification loops rather than initial generation — a structural cost dynamic foreign to SaaS mental models. Tepper argues that context management is becoming an operating asset, and that enterprises lacking cross-functional finance/engineering governance will face compounding cost disadvantages as they scale agents. The piece reframes AI scaling as primarily an economics and governance challenge, not a technical one.

💡 For investors and operators evaluating AI infrastructure moats — especially in healthcare contexts like prior auth automation or clinical ops — this cost structure insight changes the build-vs-buy calculus and surfaces a new diligence lens: who controls and optimizes context efficiently at scale.
