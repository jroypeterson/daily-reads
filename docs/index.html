What to expect in US healthcare in 2027 and beyond

McKinsey · 2026-11-22 · ⏱ 8 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

McKinsey projects US healthcare industry EBITDA growth decelerating to ~1% annually through 2027, driven by ACA subsidy expirations and Medicaid disenrollment of 7–9 million lives by 2028, creating significant margin pressure on payers and providers. Healthcare services and technology (HST) is identified as the fastest-growing segment, while AI and automation are estimated to unlock 9–15% of national health expenditure. Specific quantified upside is cited for providers (11–17% gross revenue lift) and payers (5–11% medical cost reduction) via AI-enhanced care management.

💡 Offers a data-grounded 2027 profitability roadmap across healthcare sub-sectors — identifying which segments face structural compression vs. AI-driven opportunity — directly useful for portfolio positioning across payers, providers, and HST names.
