A hidden tradeoff in Trump's drug pricing deals

BioPharma Dive · 2026-09-22 · ⏱ 5 min read

Trump's MFN pricing agreements with 26 pharma companies may undermine separate Medicare drug pricing models (GLOBE and GUARD), with one Lancet study author estimating that exempting participating companies could eliminate nearly 80% of the projected $11.6B in annual Medicare savings. The deals' actual savings impact is further muddied by the complexity of existing contractual arrangements between manufacturers, PBMs, insurers, and pharmacies, meaning lower manufacturer prices don't automatically translate to patient savings. The TrumpRx direct-to-consumer platform covers only 12% of participating companies' branded drugs, limiting its practical reach.

💡 The structural conflict between MFN exemptions and statutory Medicare pricing mechanisms creates material uncertainty for pharma revenue trajectories and managed care dynamics, making this a key policy variable for investors modeling drug pricing reform outcomes.
