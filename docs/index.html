Alphabet's Isomorphic Labs Raises $2.1 Billion for AI Drug Discovery Scale-Up

Fierce Biotech · 2026-05-20 · ⏱ 2 min read

Isomorphic Labs raised $2.1 billion in Series B funding led by Thrive Capital to scale its AI drug design engine (IsoDDE), which uses models like AlphaFold 3 to predict molecular structures and interactions. The company, founded by Alphabet in 2021 and led by Demis Hassabis, has existing partnerships with Eli Lilly and Novartis for AI-driven small molecule discovery. The funding will accelerate both platform development and the company's internal drug pipeline toward clinical trials.

💡 This represents one of the largest biotech funding rounds of 2026 and validates the AI-drug discovery thesis at massive scale, potentially signaling a new phase of institutional investment in computational biology platforms.
