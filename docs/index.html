📊 Slot 2

Humana, BlackRock strike $2.8B deal to manage CenterWell clinics

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-10-13 · ⏱ 3 min read

Humana is replacing private equity firm Welsh Carson with BlackRock's HPS Investment Partners as the joint venture partner for its ~150 CenterWell Senior Primary Care clinics in a $2.8B deal. BlackRock will buy WCAS's equity stake, with the JV structured to deploy capital over two years to operate existing clinics, open new locations, and pursue acquisitions. Humana retains the option to buy back clinics beginning in 2028, and the deal is expected to close in Q4 2025 with no impact on 2026 earnings guidance.

💡 BlackRock's second deal with Humana this year signals accelerating institutionalization of value-based care assets — a structural shift in how senior primary care is capitalized that has direct implications for managed care subsector positioning and HUM's balance sheet flexibility.
