BREAKING: mRNA Cancer Vaccine Succeeds in Late-Stage Trial

STAT News · 2026-08-23 · ⏱ 5 min read

Moderna and Merck announced that their personalized mRNA cancer vaccine (intismeran autogene) combined with Keytruda met both primary (recurrence-free survival) and secondary (distant metastasis-free survival) endpoints in a 1,137-patient Phase 3 trial in resected stage IIB–IV melanoma. This marks the first Phase 3 success for a personalized mRNA cancer vaccine, validating the neoantigen therapeutic paradigm that Moderna has been building toward since its COVID-19 platform success. Regulatory submissions are expected imminently, with full data to be presented at an upcoming oncology conference.

Who Will Win as AI Rewrites Competitive Advantage?

BCG · 2026-08-23 · ⏱ 10 min read

BCG's flagship 2026 strategy report reframes AI not as a productivity story but as a structural redistribution of value pools across industries, analogous to the railway boom or internet revolution. The report introduces a five-factor framework — task substitutability, market concentration, data defensibility, compute intensity, and regulatory friction — to explain why the same AI technology produces opposite competitive outcomes across sectors. Key findings include: legacy enterprise software has already lost ~$2 trillion in market cap, the compute layer has become a permanent new cost line for every adopter, and midscale incumbents face the most compression while true-scale data-flywheel players and AI-native attackers advance.

💡 The sector-specific value-pool analysis (pharma shareholders benefit from AI-reduced discovery costs; health care value flows to consumers; financial services oligopoly retains surplus longest) gives portfolio managers a structured lens to assess which holdings are moat-strengtheners versus slow-motion compression candidates before the P&L damage becomes visible.
