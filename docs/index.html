UniQure to File Gene Therapy for Approval, Reflecting Major Shifts at FDA

BioPharma Dive · 2026-06-18 · ⏱ 4 min read

UniQure announced it will file a BLA for its Huntington's disease gene therapy AMT-130 in Q3 2026 after the FDA reversed course and agreed that three years of Phase 1/2 data are sufficient to support accelerated approval — a dramatic shift from March when the agency publicly criticized the program's evidence. The reversal followed the departures of FDA Commissioner Makary, acting drug review director Høeg, and Vinay Prasad, who had been a vocal critic of expedited gene therapy approvals and was widely suspected to be behind the anonymous FDA briefing that hammered QURE shares earlier this year. Analysts at RBC and William Blair view the development as a positive signal not just for QURE (shares +80%) but for other rare disease gene therapy programs that faced unexpected regulatory headwinds under prior leadership.

💡 A directly actionable QURE catalyst with a defined BLA filing window, plus a leading indicator that the FDA's posture toward accelerated approval for rare disease gene therapies has meaningfully shifted, with read-through implications for other stalled programs in the sector.
