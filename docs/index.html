UPDATED: Novartis halts autoimmune CAR-T trials after 3 deaths, as BMS also pauses studies

Fierce Biotech · 2026-09-04 · ⏱ 4 min read

Novartis has halted eight autoimmune CAR-T clinical trials of rapcabtagene autoleucel (rap-cel) after three patient deaths from immune effector cell-associated hemophagocytic syndrome (IEC-HS) across indications including lupus, RA, MS, and myasthenia gravis. Bristol Myers Squibb simultaneously enacted a voluntary pause on its own autoimmune CAR-T program (zola-cel) due to transient inflammatory events. William Blair analysts highlighted that both halted programs use rapid manufacturing platforms, raising the hypothesis that accelerated manufacturing — rather than CAR-T biology in autoimmune disease broadly — may be responsible for the toxicity signal.

💡 The dual halts create meaningful pipeline risk for NVS and BMS while the manufacturing-platform hypothesis could reshape competitive dynamics in the autoimmune cell therapy space, potentially benefiting players using conventional or in-vivo CAR-T approaches such as Cabaletta Bio and Fate Therapeutics.

[WAY – Waystar] Healthcare is a very complicated invoice

Scuttleblurb · 2026-09-04 · ⏱ 8 min read

The state of AI in 2026: On the road to ROI

McKinsey · 2026-09-04 · ⏱ 8 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

McKinsey's 2026 State of AI report, drawing on 1,719 executives across 97 countries, finds 89% of organizations now deploy AI in at least one function and 44% have scaled enterprise-wide, yet only 6% qualify as true 'AI high performers' attributing 5%+ of EBIT to AI — a share flat year-over-year despite surging investment. A striking productivity paradox emerges: 80% of individual workers report gains, but only 37% of organizations see measurable EBIT impact. Agentic coding tools and tokenomics are flagged as the next frontier where high performers are pulling ahead.

💡 The stubborn ROI gap quantified here is the central valuation question for every AI-enabled SaaS and health tech company in the portfolio, offering a data-backed framework for separating genuine compounders from adoption-story hype.
