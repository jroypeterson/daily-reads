Nearly 4 Million Medicare Beneficiaries Could Be Eligible for the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program

KFF Health News · 2026-07-01 · ⏱ 3 min read

A new KFF analysis estimates 3.8 million Medicare Part D beneficiaries met eligibility criteria for the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program, which launches July 1, 2026 and runs through December 2027, covering Wegovy, Zepbound, and Foundayo at a $50/month copay. Eligibility is narrower than the 13 million with qualifying BMI thresholds because it excludes those with conditions (like type 2 diabetes) already covered under Part D and those who filled a GLP-1 prescription in 2026. Depending on participation rates of 10–75%, the federal cost ranges from $1.3 billion to $10 billion over the 18-month period.

💡 The program's launch today creates an immediate volume catalyst for Eli Lilly (LLY) and Novo Nordisk (NVO), while the cost modeling and post-2027 uncertainty around the delayed BALANCE Model are directly actionable for managed care and PBM analysis heading into Q3 earnings.

CMS launches the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge — but 4 questions linger

Becker's Hospital Review · 2026-07-01 · ⏱ 3 min read

CMS launched its Medicare GLP-1 Bridge program on July 1, with Humana serving as the central prior authorization processor — but four key operational questions remain unresolved at launch. These include workflow friction in the PA process, coverage gaps for low-income subsidy patients, the downstream plan economics impact from the delayed BALANCE Model, and unclear provider attestation liability. CMS Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz commented at the launch, but the piece focuses on execution risks rather than political framing.

💡 The unresolved operational gaps could materially affect Humana's administrative cost structure, managed care utilization forecasts, and the near-term volume ramp assumptions embedded in Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk revenue models.
