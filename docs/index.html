Hospitals' use of AI coding tools cost BCBSA plans $942M more for similar care: analysis

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-09-24 · ⏱ 5 min read

BCBSA released a white paper showing hospitals using AI revenue cycle management tools have driven a $942M increase in billed costs to Blue plans over two years, with medically complex case classifications rising from 37% to 40% since 2023 — yet with no corresponding increase in care intensity metrics like ICU utilization, transfusion rates, or length of stay. The analysis points to 'technology-enabled upcoding' via secondary diagnoses pushing claims into higher-severity DRGs, with specific examples like major bowel procedures seeing complex case share jump from 10.2% to 22.7%. BCBSA plans further analyses on outpatient care, signaling an escalating payer-provider AI arms race with regulatory and legislative implications.

💡 This is a measurable, structural flashpoint for managed care margins (UNH, CI, ELV) and hospital operators, as AI-driven coding inflation without clinical justification invites regulatory crackdown, tighter prior auth regimes, and potential CMS intervention — all near-term investable risks.

🤖 Slot 3

Claude discovers a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats

Anthropic / Hacker News · 2026-09-24 · ⏱ 6 min read

Anthropic's new life sciences research group reports that Claude, running as ~950 parallel agents over 21 hours and 210 million tokens, autonomously identified a previously uncharacterized enzyme system in bacteriophages featuring DNA repeat arrays adjacent to a reverse transcriptase gene — a structural pattern reminiscent of CRISPR. The discovery, dubbed array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART), was validated in Anthropic's own molecular biology lab and has been released as a preprint. Feng Zhang, a CRISPR pioneer at the Broad Institute, called the finding 'genuinely intriguing' and praised it as a demonstration of AI agents contributing to biological discovery.

💡 This is a concrete proof-of-concept for the AI-as-scientist thesis — Claude performing autonomous hypothesis generation and database mining that yielded a real, novel biological finding — with direct implications for the R&D cost curves of AI-native biotech platforms and the competitive positioning of any company betting on AI-accelerated drug or tool discovery.
