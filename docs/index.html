[ZTS] Zoetis could use a booster shot

Scuttleblurb · 2026-07-05 · ⏱ 12 min read

Scuttleblurb examines Zoetis (ZTS), the 2013 Pfizer spin-off that built four blockbuster pet health franchises (Simparica, Apoquel, Cytopoint, Librela) which together now represent nearly 90% of the company's total 2012 revenue base. The piece traces how ZTS's revenue mix flipped from 65% livestock to companion animal dominance, explaining why pet drugs enjoy structural advantages over both livestock and human pharma — including softer brand moats, price-insensitive consumers, and reduced generic cliff risk from a bypass of third-party payers. The analysis sets up a stress-test of the bull case following a 50%+ stock decline driven by weakening U.S. companion animal demand, guidance cuts, and competitive erosion in its key franchises.

💡 With ZTS at multi-year valuation lows, this forensic breakdown of franchise revenue concentration and moat durability provides the structural diligence needed to distinguish cyclical demand weakness from permanent competitive deterioration — directly actionable for portfolio positioning.

📊 Slot 2

Nearly 4 Million Medicare Beneficiaries Could Be Eligible for the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program

KFF Health News · 2026-07-05 · ⏱ 3 min read

A new KFF analysis estimates 3.8 million Medicare Part D beneficiaries met the eligibility criteria for the Trump administration's GLP-1 Bridge Program, which launched July 1 and covers Wegovy, Zepbound, and Foundayo through December 2027. The eligible pool is narrower than the 13M+ Medicare beneficiaries with qualifying BMI due to restrictions excluding those with diabetes or prior GLP-1 fills. Depending on participation rates (10%–75%), total federal cost ranges from $1.3 billion to $10 billion over the 18-month program.

💡 With 3.8M newly eligible beneficiaries and a program live as of this week, the volume and pricing dynamics for Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly's obesity GLP-1s are materially shifting, and the program's temporary structure makes the policy pathway to permanent Medicare coverage a key watch item for long-term demand modeling.
