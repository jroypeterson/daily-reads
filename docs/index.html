UnitedHealth faces IRS probe over potential tax avoidance

STAT News · 2026-08-17 · ⏱ 4 min read

Constellation Software 2Q'26: Soft Organic Growth, but Encouraging Deployment Pace

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-17 · ⏱ 7 min read

Constellation Software's 2Q'26 results present a tension between near-6-year-low organic growth (recurring revenue up just +2% FXN, or +4% excluding the troubled Altera unit) and an accelerating capital deployment pace, which management attributes to a cluster of idiosyncratic one-offs rather than structural deceleration. The analysis walks through the margin cohort progression — new acquisitions entering at negative margins and marching toward the high-20s/30s over years — as evidence the classic 'buy cheap and fix' playbook is intact even as large deals create near-term reported margin drag. On AI, management's framing is notable: development productivity is spreading across the portfolio, but vertical software's sales cycle means no revenue impact in either direction yet, while CSU sees potential to become a faster fast-follower if horizontal AI players validate demand in their niches.

💡 Constellation's deployment pace and deal IRR trends (e.g., Altera generating ~$400M cumulative FCF on a ~$700M purchase even as revenue shrank) are a benchmark for vertical software M&A underwriting discipline, directly informing valuation expectations for health IT serial acquirers like Waystar and the broader SMB software roll-up landscape.
