It's Not You, It's Me (This Week in Stratechery)

Stratechery · 2026-10-11 · ⏱ 8 min read

Ben Thompson's Mac Mini was compromised via an actively-exploited macOS screen-sharing vulnerability (CVE-2026-65400), and notably, his Claude Code agent detected the intrusion autonomously—stopping command execution and guiding remediation before Thompson found the public disclosure. He uses this experience to examine Apple's ambivalence toward agentic AI: macOS is uniquely well-suited as an agent host (Unix tooling, scriptability, accessibility APIs), yet Apple is tightening Full Disk Access controls in ways that may undermine that capability. The piece concludes with Thompson—for the first time—seriously contemplating moving away from Apple, framing it as a platform-loyalty inflection point driven by AI.

💡 Apple's tightening grip on Full Disk Access is a structural threat to its developer loyalty moat precisely when openness is becoming the key competitive variable in the agentic AI stack—with direct implications for AAPL's platform durability and the broader enterprise AI infrastructure landscape.
