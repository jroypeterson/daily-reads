Bon Secours Mercy Health to acquire majority stake in Surgery Partners in $5.7B deal

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-10-26 · ⏱ 4 min read

Bon Secours Mercy Health is acquiring a majority stake in Surgery Partners (SGRY) in a $5.7B take-private deal at $23.50/share, with Bain Capital rolling over its ~39% stake. The deal creates one of the largest U.S. outpatient surgical platforms and follows a similar Ascension/AmSurg transaction, reflecting a broad health system push into ambulatory care networks. The transaction is expected to close in Q1 next year pending regulatory approval.

💡 The deal is a direct M&A catalyst for SGRY and sets a valuation comp for the ASC sector, while the health-system-as-acquirer dynamic signals accelerating consolidation in outpatient surgery — a structural shift with pricing implications for independent ASC operators and adjacent platforms.
