CMS' Star Ratings Changes Could Cut MA Payments by $107B: 8 Things to Know

Becker's Payer Issues · 2026-11-23 · ⏱ 4 min read

CMS proposed a sweeping overhaul of Medicare Advantage for contract year 2028, shrinking star ratings to 15 measures with tougher methodology that would reduce federal MA payments by an estimated $107 billion over 2028–2037. The proposal also introduces a mandatory 10-year state-based payment model (ADVaNCE MA) launching in 2029, tightens network adequacy standards, restricts supplemental food benefits to nutrient-dense options, and requires FHIR-based prior authorization APIs from in-network providers. Insurer stocks reacted negatively, and the rule arrives amid ongoing litigation from at least 16 payers already contesting their 2027 star ratings.
