[ZTS] Zoetis could use a booster shot

Scuttleblurb · 2026-07-04 · ⏱ 12 min read

Scuttleblurb profiles Zoetis (ZTS), the former Pfizer animal health division, dissecting how its four blockbuster pet franchises (Simparica, Apoquel, Cytopoint, Librela) transformed it from a livestock-dominated business to a companion animal powerhouse representing a structural moat story. The piece explains why pet drugs are fundamentally superior to livestock drugs — longer treatment durations, emotional spending, brand loyalty with vets, and limited generic cliff risk — while contextualizing the current 11% U.S. companion animal sales decline in Q1 2026 against intensifying competition and pet-owner price sensitivity. The analysis frames the core investment question: whether Zoetis' durable competitive advantages justify its valuation amid near-term headwinds.

A behind-the-scenes look at Midjourney's medical scanner leaves many questions unanswered

The Verge · 2026-07-04 · ⏱ 4 min read

Midjourney released a behind-the-scenes video of its full-body ultrasound scanner, described by an internal engineer as ultrasound probes 'hacked apart and slapped on a glorified hot tub' running on off-the-shelf hardware and Raspberry Pis. The company is positioning it as a wellness/body-composition product to avoid FDA clearance requirements, but continues to use medical language and aspirational claims about transforming diagnostics. Experts previously told The Verge that Midjourney has offered little evidence it can overcome fundamental limits of ultrasound physics, and the new video does little to address those concerns.
