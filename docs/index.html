Elevance moves to require site-of-care details on hospital claims

Becker's Payer Issues · 2026-09-29 · ⏱ 3 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

Elevance Health is requiring hospitals to include physical site-of-care details on billing claims, enabling payment adjustments for off-campus outpatient services across commercial, Medicare Advantage, and Medicaid lines. The policy targets the broader trend of hospitals billing at higher facility rates for services delivered at off-campus locations. This represents a structural reimbursement tightening move with meaningful financial implications for hospital systems and ambulatory surgery center operators.

💡 ELV's policy directly pressures hospital outpatient revenue by closing a billing arbitrage gap, creating near-term headwinds for hospital operators and a potential tailwind for ASC-focused players — a concrete, investable payer dynamic.
