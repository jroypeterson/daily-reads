Pfizer strikes up to $10.5B cancer drug deal with China's Innovent Biologics

BioPharma Dive · 2026-05-30 · ⏱ 3 min read

Pfizer struck a potentially $10.5 billion partnership with China's Innovent Biologics covering 12 cancer drug programs, with $650M upfront and up to $9.85B in milestones. The deal focuses on next-generation antibody-drug conjugates and multispecific antibodies, with Innovent handling early discovery and Pfizer taking over global development after Phase 1. This represents Pfizer's largest bet on China-discovered medicines and is part of a broader trend of major pharma companies forming multibillion-dollar partnerships with Chinese biotechs.

💡 This deal exemplifies the strategic shift toward China as a key source of drug innovation, potentially reshaping global pharmaceutical development while raising questions about US competitive positioning in biotech.
