Optum Health CEO pulls back curtain on turnaround

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-09-10 · ⏱ 3 min read

She disclosed that the 'vast majority' of misaligned payer contracts for 2027 have been addressed, and that the company plans to sell certain Florida clinic interests to TPG while remaining committed to the state.
