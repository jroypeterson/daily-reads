Akeso's ivonescimab achieves landmark 34% reduction in death risk for lung cancer at ASCO 2026

ASCO 2026 / Multiple Healthcare Sources · 2026-06-01 · ⏱ 6 min read

Akeso's ivonescimab achieved a statistically significant 34% reduction in death risk versus standard PD-1 plus chemotherapy in advanced squamous non-small cell lung cancer, marking the first China-originated drug to reach ASCO's prestigious plenary session. While the overall survival benefit exceeded expectations, oncology experts raised questions about follow-up duration, patient selection, and global applicability of the China-only dataset.

💡 This validates the dual PD-1/VEGF mechanism in a $20B+ lung cancer market and could establish a new treatment paradigm, though expert skepticism about data maturity and generalizability creates both opportunity and risk for investors evaluating the China biotech sector.
