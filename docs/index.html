STAT+ FDA Expert Committee Reviews Capricor Therapeutics' Duchenne Drug (Live Blog)

STAT News · 2026-07-29 · ⏱ 5 min read

The FDA's Cellular, Tissue, and Gene Therapies Advisory Committee is meeting July 29 to review Capricor Therapeutics' BLA for deramiocel, an allogeneic cardiosphere-derived cell therapy for cardiomyopathy in Duchenne muscular dystrophy patients. The review follows a contentious regulatory history including a prior complete response letter that was later rescinded, and is supported by Phase 3 HOPE-3 data showing 54% slowing of disease progression on the primary endpoint along with supportive cardiac function data. The PDUFA date is August 22, 2026, making the adcomm vote a critical near-term signal for approval odds.

💡 A positive adcomm recommendation would be a major binary catalyst for CAPR stock with the FDA decision only ~24 days away, and a favorable outcome would also establish meaningful regulatory precedent for allogeneic cell therapies in rare neuromuscular diseases.

📊 Slot 2

ACA Subsidy Fallout Boosts Insurers, Pressures Hospitals' Payer Mix

Becker's Payer Issues · 2026-07-29 · ⏱ 5 min read

The expiration of ACA enhanced premium tax credits at end-2025 is creating a bifurcated earnings landscape: hospital operators like HCA (now guiding to $1.0–1.2B in full-year headwinds, up from $600–900M), CHS, and UHS are absorbing a near 1-for-1 shift from exchange patients to uninsured self-pay, while insurers like Centene (MLR improved from 90.6% to 79.2%) and Elevance are reporting margin outperformance as healthier members dropped coverage and improved their remaining risk pools. Tenet was a partial outlier, offsetting a 17% exchange revenue decline through cost initiatives and ambulatory surgery mix, though it too faces volume pressure. Nationwide ACA enrollment fell 12% to 19.2M by February, with the sickest patients disproportionately remaining insured.

💡 The divergence between hospital operators absorbing uninsured volume hits and managed care insurers benefiting from favorable risk selection is the most material near-term earnings driver in healthcare services, creating a clear relative-value trade between names like HCA/UHS on one side and CNC/ELV on the other heading into the back half of 2026.
