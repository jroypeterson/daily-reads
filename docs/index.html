Freenome colorectal cancer test data improve on earlier results

BioPharma Dive · 2026-07-14 · ⏱ 3 min read

Freenome's updated SimpleScreen CRC blood test achieved 80.4% sensitivity for colorectal cancer and 18.2% for advanced precancerous lesions in a pivotal validation study, improving on its first-generation test. Abbott, which acquired Exact Sciences (and its Cologuard stool test) for $21B, holds exclusive commercialization rights and will owe Freenome a $70M milestone payment contingent on FDA approval of the next-gen test. The first-gen PMA is under FDA review with a mid-2026 expected completion, while a supplemental PMA for the updated test will follow — putting Abbott on a collision course with Guardant Health's already-approved Shield blood test.

Digital health funding hits $7.4B in 2026 as AI investment reshapes the market

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-07-14 · ⏱ 5 min read

Rock Health's H1 2026 report shows U.S. digital health VC reached $7.4B across 244 deals, up $1B from H1 2025, with capital heavily concentrated — 19 companies capturing 45% of all funding via megadeals of $100M+. Chai Discovery, an A.I. Drug Start-Up, Raises $400 Million

New York Times · 2026-07-14 · ⏱ 4 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

Chai Discovery has raised $400M at a $3.8B valuation, nearly tripling its $1.3B valuation from just seven months ago, backed by OpenAI and partnered with Eli Lilly and Pfizer on its Chai-2 foundation model for antibody and small molecule design. Unlike competitors Isomorphic Labs and Xaira, Chai operates as a pure platform — licensing model access rather than owning drug assets — a structural bet that AI infrastructure, not drug development risk, is the value driver. The round is the second-largest AI biotech raise in H1 2026, underscoring accelerating capital concentration in AI-native drug discovery.

💡 The pure-platform business model distinction and rapid valuation step-up make this directly relevant to the reader's AI-biology intersection and VC insights theses, offering a lens into how institutional investors are pricing AI drug discovery infrastructure versus asset-owning competitors.
