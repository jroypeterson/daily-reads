The 10-Point: Private Credit Stress Mounts as Returns Fall

WSJ Newsletters · 2026-08-10 · ⏱ 5 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

WSJ investigates a growing disconnect between private credit managers' optimistic marketing and the actual health of underlying loans, with Fitch data showing U.S. private credit default rates hitting 6% in April 2026 — with distressed exchanges masking the true severity. PIK loan usage has nearly doubled to 11% of the market, a classic sign of borrower stress being deferred rather than resolved. Major BDCs are trading below NAV and semi-liquid fund redemption queues are lengthening, raising structural liquidity concerns across a $1.7–2T asset class.
