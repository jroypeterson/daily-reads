In autopsy of failed heart disease study, AstraZeneca raises broader questions about silencer drugs

STAT News · 2026-08-28 · ⏱ 6 min read

[scuttleslops] AI Data Center Economics, ADI, INTU, IDXX

Scuttleblurb · 2026-08-28 · ⏱ 6 min read

A former AWS data center executive breaks down the ROI challenges of hyperscaler AI campus builds, estimating fully loaded one-gigawatt deployments at $40–$45B upfront with base-case IRRs of 15–20%, potentially rising to 35–40% by 2028 as inference scales. He details the cost stack across land/power infrastructure (~$18–23B) and accelerators/networking (~$25–30B), noting the industry is paying 30–40% premiums for power to avoid idle GPU depreciation. AWS's custom silicon roadmap — particularly Trainium 3 — is framed as the key lever to materially improve compute-per-megawatt and thus the return on already-committed land and power.

💡 Rare practitioner unit economics on hyperscaler AI infrastructure provide actionable context for evaluating the sustainability of AI capex cycles and the competitive moats of custom silicon plays like AWS Trainium versus NVIDIA — directly relevant to positions in NVDA, ADI, and cloud infrastructure broadly.

Nvidia's Long-Term Margin Question

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-28 · ⏱ 10 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

Published coinciding with Nvidia's earnings, MBI Deep Dives dissects whether Nvidia's ~63–75% gross margins are structurally defensible as AI workloads shift from training to inference and hyperscalers (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta) accelerate custom silicon development. The piece contends that buy-side expectations already embed a materially worse long-run margin trajectory than sell-side consensus, meaning Nvidia can 'climb the wall of worries' even as structural margin compression risks grow. With ~70% FY28 revenue growth guidance implying ~$700B in revenue, the central question is how much of today's pricing power persists when inference commoditizes and custom ASICs scale.

💡 For investors with exposure to AI infrastructure, this is a disciplined framework for stress-testing Nvidia's long-term competitive moat against the consensus, directly relevant to position sizing and valuation assumptions.
