Hyperscalers vs AI Labs Debate, and the Moving AI Frontier

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-03 · ⏱ 8 min read

MBI's analyst uses Microsoft's Q2 2026 earnings as a launchpad to examine the structural power dynamics between hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) and frontier AI labs, arguing that as labs mature and gain the ability to self-build compute infrastructure, their bargaining power over cloud landlords will increase—compressing hyperscaler margins toward a 'convenience premium.' The piece grapples with counterarguments around AWS's lack of internal compute sinks (unlike Google's 1P workloads), the composition of contracted backlog being more lab-heavy than current revenue mix, and real-world constraints like power availability that could preserve hyperscaler leverage. The author concludes with deliberate ambivalence, noting current multiples leave little room for error and favoring an observational stance over the next few quarters.

💡 For investors sizing positions in MSFT, GOOG, and AMZN, this piece crystallizes the core long-term risk—whether hyperscalers are durable infrastructure landlords or transitional middlemen—and offers a framework for thinking about when and how AI lab bargaining power might actually show up in financials.
