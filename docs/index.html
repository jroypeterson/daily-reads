Breaking: Epic's alleged anti-competitive practices under FTC scrutiny

STAT News · 2026-08-16 · ⏱ 5 min read

The FTC is conducting a broad antitrust inquiry into Epic Systems, the dominant EHR vendor whose software covers providers treating over 280 million Americans, probing alleged data-access restrictions and NDA practices that lock healthcare organizations in and shut out rivals. Multiple state attorneys general are joining the inquiry, and a Texas AG lawsuit already alleges Epic holds patient data hostage to foreclose competition. The investigation is still early-stage but signals intensifying regulatory scrutiny of Epic's platform control, which could force data-portability mandates that reshape the entire health IT ecosystem.

Constellation Software 2Q'26: Soft Organic Growth, but Encouraging Deployment Pace

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-16 · ⏱ 7 min read

Constellation Software's Q2 2026 printed its weakest organic recurring revenue growth (~+2% FXN) in nearly six years, driven by a confluence of one-offs including tough comps at Altera and Dark Matter, a large customer churn in South America, and Lumine digesting acquisitions — management argues normalized organic growth remains ~5%. Capital deployment is running hot, with the 2026 acquisition cohort already swinging from -16% to +16% EBITA margins within a single quarter, illustrating the classic CSU buy-cheap-and-fix playbook. On AI, management's message is consistent: development productivity gains are spreading across the portfolio but revenue impact — positive or negative — remains absent, with the notable insight that AI could accelerate CSU's ability to fast-follow horizontal AI players into its verticals.

💡 For investors tracking whether the decentralized serial-acquirer model scales at CSU's size, this quarter's Rorschach-test dynamic — soft organic growth explained away as transient mix-shift versus structural friction from larger, lower-growth acquisitions — is exactly the tension that determines long-term compounder thesis integrity.
