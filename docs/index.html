Constellation Software 2Q'26: Soft Organic Growth, but Encouraging Deployment Pace

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-14 · ⏱ 8 min read

Constellation Software's Q2 2026 saw organic recurring revenue growth fall to +2% FXN — the weakest in ~6 years — driven by idiosyncratic headwinds at Altera, Dark Matter, Lumine, and a South American customer churn event; management argues normalized organic growth is still ~5%. Capital deployment, however, is running hot at ~$2.5B in 7.5 months (surpassing all of 2025), with larger deals like DerbySoft (~4x revenue) signaling a deliberate shift toward bigger, messier acquisitions that depress near-term reported margins but follow CSU's proven 'buy cheap and fix it' playbook. On AI, management offered a nuanced vertical SaaS perspective: productivity gains are real internally, but revenue impact is nil in either direction because selling — not building — is the hard part in vertical market software.

💡 For anyone benchmarking roll-up strategies or underwriting vertical SaaS moats, this analysis clarifies the structural tension between CSU's decelerating organic growth and accelerating M&A pace — and whether the canonical serial acquirer thesis remains intact at scale.
