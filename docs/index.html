After a cancer vaccine's first success, the race is on to expand the impact

STAT News · 2026-10-25 · ⏱ 7 min read

STAT goes beyond the headline data to examine what the result means for the mRNA platform's expansion into other tumor types, the manufacturing and neoantigen selection challenges that remain, and how the adjuvant immunotherapy paradigm may compete or coexist with neoadjuvant approaches. The piece contextualizes the competitive landscape and the scientific hurdles still separating melanoma success from broader oncology application.

[ISRG – Intuitive Surgical] the robot is the easy part

Scuttleblurb · 2026-10-25 · ⏱ 18 min read

Scuttleblurb traces Intuitive Surgical's origin from a DARPA-era battlefield telesurgery concept to its accidental product-procedure fit in prostatectomy, explaining how the business model evolved into a razor-razorblade annuity stream in which each installed robot generates ~7 years of instrument and service recurring revenue. The piece details how procedure volume — not hardware sales — drives all three revenue lines (systems, instruments, services), and how early dominance in prostatectomy (~67% share by 2010) and cancer hysterectomy (~80% by 2014) created the installed base and surgeon training lock-in that underpins ISRG's durable competitive moat. The excerpt sets up what is clearly a longer analysis of competitive threats from MDT, SYK, and J&J, and TAM expansion from da Vinci 5's new cardiac clearances.
