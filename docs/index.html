How Trump's cuts are reshaping America's health system

STAT News · 2026-08-25 · ⏱ 7 min read

STAT News opens its 'Unraveled' investigative series with a ground-level examination of how Maine's rural health systems — among the most Medicaid-dependent in the country — are restructuring operations in anticipation of $1 trillion in Medicaid cuts over the next decade. Northern Light Health and MaineHealth are shifting care to lower-cost settings, accelerating AI adoption, building Medicaid navigation capacity, and contemplating service closures and consolidations to offset projected revenue losses. Maine serves as a national 'canary in the coal mine,' with hospitals operating on sub-1% margins facing potential tipping into losses from even a 1% revenue decline.

💡 Medicaid funding cuts represent the most consequential near-term policy risk for hospital operators, managed care payers, and rural health service companies — this piece provides specific, actionable intelligence on how systems are adapting operationally and financially before the cuts fully materialize.

🌀 Slot 4

Who Should Control Anthropic?

Bloomberg (Money Stuff) · 2026-08-25 · ⏱ 8 min read

Matt Levine dissects Anthropic's unusual hybrid governance structure, which layers a Public Benefit Corporation with a Long-Term Benefit Trust designed to represent humanity's interests rather than shareholders', while founders retain significant control rights. The piece analyzes the tensions between profit-seeking investors (Google, Amazon, and others who have poured in billions), the mission-driven trust, and the founding team as Anthropic approaches a potential landmark IPO. Levine draws parallels to dual-class share structures and questions whether public markets can accommodate a company whose stated governance priority is explicitly not shareholder returns.

💡 As Anthropic approaches a potential record IPO, understanding its governance architecture is essential for investors evaluating AI infrastructure exposure — the structure could set precedent for how frontier AI companies handle the tension between mission and capital returns.
