Overcoming the R&D Bottleneck: Building a Causal Decision System

McKinsey · 2026-11-02 · ⏱ 7 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

McKinsey outlines a framework for deploying causal AI and decision-science systems to address the persistent R&D productivity crisis in biopharma, where high attrition rates and rising spend have failed to improve outcomes. The approach moves beyond correlation-based predictive models toward causal inference applied to trial design, portfolio prioritization, and go/no-go decision gates. 💡 Causal decision systems in pharma R&D represent a durable investment theme with direct implications for CRO valuations, AI drug discovery platform companies, and how large biopharma allocates capital across its pipeline.
