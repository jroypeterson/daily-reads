[WAY – Waystar] Healthcare is a very complicated invoice

Scuttleblurb · 2026-09-07 · ⏱ 12 min read

Scuttleblurb provides a deep-dive into Waystar (WAY), examining the extraordinary complexity of US healthcare billing — where ~18% of claims are initially denied, procedures carry dozens of different prices across payers, and administrative costs consume 15-25% of total healthcare spend. The piece dissects how Waystar's clearinghouse network and AI-powered denial management tools sit at the center of the claim-to-cash (RCM) workflow, processing a data-rich, high-stakes process that creates meaningful switching costs. The analysis frames Waystar's competitive moat as a function of network effects, workflow embeddedness, and the structural intractability of the billing problem itself.

💡 For investors, this is the definitive independent moat assessment of WAY at a pivotal moment in the AI-RCM narrative, offering actionable insight into whether Waystar's competitive position is durable or vulnerable to disruption.

Bypassing RevenueBench to Buy the Feedback Loop

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-09-07 · ⏱ 5 min read

MBI Deep Dives introduces 'RevenueBench' as the emerging true benchmark for AI model quality — real-world wallet share — while arguing traditional benchmarks are becoming noisy and saturated. Meta is deliberately bypassing RevenueBench by giving away its latest models free in exchange for developer training data, rapidly capturing ~40% of tokens on OpenCode (5 trillion tokens/day), approaching twice Google's internal coding harness volume. This feedback loop strategy lets Meta compound model quality without charging for it, while also reducing its ~$10B annual spend on Anthropic and OpenAI APIs.

💡 For investors, this reframes the AI competitive moat question away from benchmark performance toward data flywheel accumulation — with direct implications for Meta's long-term AI cost structure, Anthropic's revenue durability, and the viability of frontier model pricing power.
