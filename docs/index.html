Humana, UnitedHealthcare received $178M in combined Medicare Advantage overpayments: OIG

Becker's Payer Issues · 2026-09-16 · ⏱ 3 min read

The HHS Office of Inspector General published audit reports finding Humana and UnitedHealthcare received a combined $177.8 million in Medicare Advantage overpayments, with error rates of 81% and 73% respectively across sampled diagnosis codes unsupported by medical records. The pattern was strikingly consistent across both plans — patients who had survived strokes or cancer were coded as still having active conditions. Both insurers contested the findings and rejected the OIG's recommendations, challenging extrapolation methodology and arguing the audits were structurally one-sided; the OIG rejected all objections, though CMS retains final authority on recoupment.

💡 With MA profitability already under pressure from elevated utilization and CMS rate headwinds, these OIG findings — showing systematic, high-error-rate coding practices at HUM and UNH — signal escalating audit and clawback risk that could further compress MA margins across the managed care sector.
