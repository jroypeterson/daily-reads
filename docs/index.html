Medtech needs more than a market rotation to win back investors

McKinsey · 2026-10-18 · ⏱ 6 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

McKinsey finds medtech has delivered only ~3% annual TSR from 2018–2025 versus 12% for the S&P 500, and argues the gap won't close through sector rotation alone. The analysis benchmarks medtech against nine other industries across seven fundamental metrics, identifying structural deficits in innovation-led growth, portfolio discipline, and capital allocation. 📊 Slot 2

some thoughts on FICO

Scuttleblurb · 2026-10-18 · ⏱ 12 min read

Scuttleblurb revisits FICO after a ~58% drawdown driven by FHFA's approval of VantageScore 4.0 for GSE mortgage underwriting, which compressed FICO's multiple from ~63x to ~20x trailing earnings. The piece systematically walks through the full mortgage securitization chain — originators, GSEs, private-label issuers, mortgage insurers, rating agencies — to assess which of FICO's eight-plus constituency groups could realistically migrate away and where the genuine moat endpoints lie. The analysis distinguishes between constituency groups where switching is theoretically possible versus practically constrained, framing the current selloff as potentially panic-driven multiple compression rather than a fundamental moat breach.

💡 FICO is one of the most contested competitive-moat situations in public markets right now, and this practitioner-level dissection of the securitization stack provides the structural framework an investor needs to assess whether the drawdown represents durable impairment or a mean-reversion opportunity — directly actionable for portfolio positioning.
