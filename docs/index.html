Prior authorization disputes reach another legal boiling point

STAT News · 2026-09-19 · ⏱ 6 min read

HCA Healthcare's Texas hospitals have filed suit against Independence Blue Cross over more than $345 million in denied claims spanning four years, centering on disputes over 'medical necessity' standards applied by an out-of-state payer to in-network care. The case exposes a systemic tension between large hospital systems and commercial insurers, with HCA alleging Blue Cross applied inappropriate criteria to deny or underpay legitimate claims. Separately, CMS's AI-assisted WISeR prior authorization pilot, launched in January 2026, has itself drawn scrutiny after documented instances of delayed patient care, complicating the narrative around AI as a solution to prior authorization burdens.

Groceries, Aggregators, and Agents

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-09-19 · ⏱ 12 min read

MBI Deep Dives traces Walmart's rapid ascent to US grocery leadership after entering in 1988, then analyzes how pandemic-driven behavioral shifts are replacing weekly stock-up trips with frequent fill-in deliveries — benefiting demand aggregators like DoorDash and Instacart. The piece then extrapolates to how LLM-powered AI agents could fundamentally disintermediate today's search and discovery aggregators, drawing a parallel to Walmart's own supply chain verticalization as a model for thinking about durable platform moats. The author argues that whoever controls the agent layer — whether retailers, marketplaces, or new entrants — will capture the rent currently extracted by aggregators.

💡 The AI-agent-as-aggregator-disruptor thesis is a high-conviction structural insight with broad investable implications across retail platforms, digital health navigation, and any business whose moat depends on being the discovery or comparison layer between consumers and suppliers.
