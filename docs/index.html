Novo Nordisk inflammation-targeting drug misses mark in heart disease study

STAT News · 2026-08-01 · ⏱ 3 min read

Novo Nordisk's ziltivekimab, an IL-6 inhibitor tested in over 6,300 patients with heart disease, kidney disease, and elevated inflammation, failed its pivotal ZEUS Phase 3 cardiovascular outcomes trial. Despite successfully lowering inflammation markers, the drug did not reduce the risk of major cardiovascular events including death, heart attack, or stroke. The failure raises significant questions about the broader hypothesis that targeting inflammation can prevent cardiovascular complications — a thesis multiple companies have staked pipeline assets on.

💡 The ZEUS trial failure is a sector-wide repricing event: it challenges the inflammation-as-cardio-target hypothesis and may negatively impact pipeline valuations at AstraZeneca, Agepha, and other companies with similar mechanistic bets, while simultaneously creating potential short opportunities and forcing portfolio reassessment across cardiovascular drug development.
