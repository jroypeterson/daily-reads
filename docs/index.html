Senate strikes down Dems move to overturn CMS' WISeR AI prior auth pilot

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-07-19 · ⏱ 3 min read

The Senate voted 46-50 along party lines to reject a Democratic resolution that would have rolled back CMS's WISeR model, a year-long, six-state pilot using AI to review prior authorizations for 13 Medicare services deemed low-value or misuse-prone. The vote, triggered after the GAO determined WISeR was subject to Congressional Review Act oversight, allows the program to continue operating in Arizona, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Texas, and Washington. Provider groups including the AHA and AMA have raised concerns, and Washington state data showed procedure completion times doubling to quadrupling after implementation.

💡 The WISeR pilot's survival is a pivotal regulatory precedent — the first federally sanctioned AI-driven coverage decision system in traditional Medicare — directly affecting managed care payers, prior-auth technology vendors, and investors tracking AI's expanding role in healthcare reimbursement.
