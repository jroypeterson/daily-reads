Merck KGaA inks $11.3B Bio-Techne buyout to expand into cell therapy production

BioSpace · 2026-06-30 · ⏱ 3 min read

Merck KGaA has agreed to acquire Bio-Techne (NASDAQ: TECH) for $11.3 billion ($73/share, a 36% premium to one-month VWAP), its largest life sciences deal since the 2015 Sigma-Aldrich acquisition. The deal targets Bio-Techne's consumables-heavy portfolio (81% of revenue) used in drug discovery, development, and advanced therapy manufacturing — particularly cell therapy, which is growing at 20%+ annually. Merck KGaA plans to leverage its global footprint to expand Bio-Techne's historically Americas-weighted revenue base, and expects ~€140M in annual cost synergies within three years, with no revenue synergies assumed in the model.

💡 The deal sets a valuation benchmark for life science tools platforms and confirms that cell therapy manufacturing infrastructure remains a high-priority strategic acquisition target for large-cap pharma, with direct implications for peers in the coverage universe.

25 states sue Trump admin over Medicaid work requirements — 8 things to know

Becker's Payer Issues · 2026-06-30 · ⏱ 4 min read

Twenty-five state AGs and D.C. filed a 74-page federal lawsuit challenging CMS's interim final rule on Medicaid work requirements, arguing it exceeds statutory authority and bypassed notice-and-comment procedures. Implementation is set to begin in Montana and Arkansas on July 1, making the preliminary injunction timeline a critical near-term catalyst. The outcome carries major enrollment and revenue implications for managed care plans, safety-net hospitals, and behavioral health facilities serving Medicaid populations.

💡 A ruling — or preliminary injunction — in this case is the single biggest near-term enrollment risk for managed care (CNC, CI, UNH), hospital (CYH, THC), and behavioral health (UHS) companies, making it a high-priority regulatory catalyst to monitor.
