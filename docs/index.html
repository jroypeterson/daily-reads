🌀 Slot 4

[CoStar] Homes are different

Scuttleblurb · 2026-09-27 · ⏱ 12 min read

Scuttleblurb examines CoStar's attempt to replicate its Apartments.com and LoopNet playbook — buy a distressed listing brand, enrich it with proprietary data, flood it with marketing capital, then harvest via premium tiers — in the far more competitive single-family residential market via Homes.com. The piece details CoStar's $1B+ investment in Homes.com, its differentiated 'Your Listing, Your Lead' model that routes inquiries to seller's agents rather than buyer's agents (flipping Zillow's model), and the structural question of whether the same data-moat advantages that produced $1.3B in Apartments revenue at 40%+ EBITDA margins can translate to a market where Zillow is deeply entrenched. The analysis interrogates the key asymmetry: CoStar's cash-generative commercial data business subsidizes an aggressive listing land-grab, but residential homes may be different enough in consumer behavior and agent economics to resist the formula.

💡 For a reader focused on data infrastructure compounders and competitive moat analysis, this is a live case study in whether proprietary data assets and capital patience can displace a dominant incumbent — with real investment implications for both CSGP and Z.
