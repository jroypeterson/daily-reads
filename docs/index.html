Cancer, immune drugmakers dominate VC funding so far in 2026

BioPharma Dive · 2026-07-06 · ⏱ 3 min read

BioPharma Dive's proprietary data from 26 active investors shows oncology and immunology startups captured over $3.9 billion — more than 40% — of the $9B+ in biotech VC deployed in H1 2026, with immune disease alone drawing $2.1B. Key rounds include Isomorphic Labs ($2.1B, AI drug discovery), Beeline Medicines ($426M+, immune), and Parabilis Medicine ($305M Series F, oncology), with notable participation from tech-crossover investors like Google and Kleiner Perkins. Analysts flag patent cliff dynamics and pharma's appetite for pipeline optionality — particularly T-cell engagers bridging oncology and immunology — as catalysts for continued M&A activity in both sectors.
