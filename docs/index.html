Novartis RNA Drug Falls Short in Rare Muscle Disease Study ($12B Avidity Flop)

BioPharma Dive · 2026-09-08 · ⏱ 4 min read

Novartis's del-desiran, the lead asset from its $12B Avidity Biosciences acquisition, failed its Phase 3 HARBOR trial in myotonic dystrophy type 1, missing the primary endpoint of video hand-opening time. The failure — Novartis's third clinical setback in a week — sent shares down over 13% and prompted Jefferies to question both the AOC platform thesis and Novartis's M&A due diligence, given that alternative DM1 approaches were available at the time of acquisition. Dyne Therapeutics also fell 22% on read-through risk, with Cantor Fitzgerald cutting its probability of success on Dyne's DM1 candidate from 50% to 25% due to shared endpoint variability concerns.

💡 The failure undermines the antibody-oligonucleotide conjugate platform thesis that underpinned a $12B bet and reshuffles DM1 competitive dynamics, with direct valuation implications for RNA/oligo platform companies across the sector and pressure on Novartis to accelerate further M&A ahead of its 2030 patent cliff.

AlphaGenome Atlas: Predictive Map of Every DNA Letter Change in the Human Genome

Google DeepMind (via Hacker News / blog.google) · 2026-09-08 · ⏱ 3 min read

Google DeepMind has launched AlphaGenome Atlas, a free 1-petabyte database pre-computing the predicted molecular effects of all 9 billion possible single-nucleotide variants in the human genome, covering the previously intractable 98% noncoding region. The platform introduces an AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score to help researchers prioritize variants without coding skills, and early results show a 22% increase in non-coding genetic associations discovered in 54,000+ UK Biobank participants. Real-world applications already include solving rare disease cases at the Broad Institute and identifying 19 BMI-linked genetic regions.

💡 This is a foundational AI-biology infrastructure event that dramatically lowers the cost of noncoding variant interpretation, accelerating target identification and rare disease diagnostics for precision medicine and genomics-driven drug discovery companies across the coverage universe.
