Hims & Hers inks deal with Eli Lilly to offer branded weight loss drugs

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-11-07 · ⏱ 4 min read

Hims & Hers has partnered with Eli Lilly to offer branded GLP-1 medications Zepbound ($299/mo) and Foundayo ($149/mo) directly on its platform, mirroring its March deal with Novo Nordisk and completing its pivot away from compounded medications. The agreement gives Hims pharmacies direct access to Lilly's FDA-approved obesity drugs at LillyDirect self-pay prices, eliminating the previous friction of patients having to leave the platform. The transition has come at a financial cost — gross margins have compressed to ~63% and the company posted an $86 million loss in Q2 — with Q3 earnings now a key readout for branded-drug margin trajectory.

💡 The dual Lilly/Novo branded GLP-1 partnerships resolve HIMS's major strategic overhang and set up Q3 as a critical catalyst for assessing whether lower-margin branded distribution can offset the loss of high-margin compounding revenue, with direct competitive implications vs. Ro and direct pharma-to-consumer channels.

The AI 'credit card' is getting costlier as CoreWeave bond yield tops 16%

CNBC Finance · 2026-11-07 · ⏱ 4 min read

CoreWeave's 2032 bond now yields above 16% and its five-year CDS spiked to a record 1,289 basis points, while CDS on Nvidia (+76%), Broadcom (+49%), and Amazon (+44%) have also risen sharply over the past month. The trigger is political: incoming Democratic governors in Texas, Ohio, and Georgia could impose moratoriums affecting up to 45% of planned U.S. data center capacity, per Goldman Sachs. BofA projects AI-related debt issuance peaks at $685B in 2027 before declining, with the 'Blue Wave' regulatory risk cited as the primary downside.

💡 CoreWeave's credit stress is a leading indicator for the entire AI infrastructure capital cycle — if neocloud debt financing deteriorates, it flows upstream into GPU demand assumptions, hyperscaler capex sustainability, and ultimately Nvidia's revenue outlook.
