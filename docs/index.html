Vertex to acquire Crinetics in $10B foray into endocrine disease drugs

BioPharma Dive · 2026-07-07 · ⏱ 4 min read

Vertex Pharmaceuticals agreed to acquire Crinetics Pharmaceuticals for $85/share in cash (~$10B total), a 102% premium, marking Vertex's largest-ever deal and its entry into endocrine disorders. Crinetics brings PALSONIFY (paltusotine), an FDA-approved oral acromegaly therapy with early promising launch metrics, plus atumelnant, a late-stage CAH candidate competing against Neurocrine's already-launched Crenessity. Vertex projects the two assets could generate over $5B in combined peak annual sales, though analysts flag the rich valuation and execution risk given competition from Pfizer, Novartis, and Neurocrine.

💡 The deal signals Vertex's aggressive pipeline diversification strategy beyond cystic fibrosis and sets a high M&A multiple benchmark for rare endocrine disease assets, with direct implications for sector valuation and competitive dynamics in the specialty rare-disease space.

CMS floats new Medicare payment category for AI diagnostic software: 6 notes

Becker's Hospital Review · 2026-07-07 · ⏱ 3 min read

CMS released a proposed rule on July 2 that would create a first-ever dedicated Medicare payment category for AI-driven diagnostic software within the 2027 Hospital Outpatient Prospective Payment System. The proposal outlines eligibility criteria, scope, and a comment timeline, representing a significant regulatory shift that has been long-awaited by the health AI industry. If finalized, this structured reimbursement framework would address the primary commercial barrier — lack of predictable payment — that has constrained revenue growth for AI diagnostic companies.

💡 A defined CMS reimbursement pathway is potentially the most important commercial unlock for AI diagnostics, and investors in health AI or digital health companies should assess which players are best positioned to capture reimbursement under the proposed eligibility criteria.
