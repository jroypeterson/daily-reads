🧬 Slot 1

Summit shares fall as FDA rejects closely watched PD-1/VEGF drug

BioPharma Dive · 2026-11-16 · ⏱ 4 min read

The FDA issued a Complete Response Letter refusing to approve Summit Therapeutics' ivonescimab for EGFR-mutant NSCLC, citing the drug's failure to demonstrate overall survival benefit in the HARMONi-A trial — a PFS-only readout the agency deemed insufficient. The rejection is Summit's first failed U.S. approval attempt and signals that the FDA will require OS data, not just progression-free survival, as the evidentiary bar for this drug class. The decision creates immediate read-across uncertainty for rival PD-1xVEGF bispecific programs from Akeso, BioNTech, and others that are still in earlier-stage trials.

💡 The FDA's explicit OS requirement resets the competitive and regulatory timeline for the entire PD-1xVEGF bispecific class, directly affecting Summit's valuation and creating a framework investors must apply to all downstream programs in this space.

Modeling Alphabet, Part 1: Search, Gemini, and YouTube

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-11-16 · ⏱ 12 min read

MBI Deep Dives builds a granular financial model of Alphabet's Google Services segment, projecting Google's search query share falling from ~76% today to ~52% by 2035 as AI assistants capture a growing slice of the query pie. Despite that share loss, the author argues Search revenue can still grow meaningfully because total search-like queries are expanding ~16-19% annually — people ask AI things they'd never have typed into a search box. The piece dissects paid clicks per query (declining ~4%/yr), cost per click (~3-3.5% growth), and the role of Gemini app ad monetization as the key swing factor.

💡 For an investor weighing Alphabet's AI-disruption risk versus its structural ad moat, this model quantifies the range of outcomes in Search revenue through 2035 and identifies the specific variables — assistant query share ceiling and paid clicks per query recovery — that will determine whether GOOG compounds or stagnates.
