Replimune melanoma drug wins support of FDA panel

BioPharma Dive · 2026-07-31 · ⏱ 3 min read

An FDA advisory committee voted 10-3 on July 30 that efficacy data from Replimune's RP1 (vusolimogene oderparepvec) plus nivolumab in advanced melanoma were 'evaluable and clinically meaningful,' overriding FDA staff skepticism about the single-arm trial design and survival analysis reliability. The favorable panel vote dramatically increases the probability of approval after two prior rejections, with the FDA's binding PDUFA action date set for August 2. The article details both the bull case (unmet need, meaningful response/CR rates, ongoing confirmatory trial) and the bear case (no control arm, difficulty attributing benefit to RP1 vs. Opdivo, unreliable responder/non-responder survival comparison).

💡 With the PDUFA date on August 2, this is an immediate binary catalyst where the adcom vote materially shifts the approval probability, making REPL one of the most time-sensitive investable events in the healthcare space right now.
