PhRMA Sues Over Trump Policy Mandating Lower Drug Prices in Medicare

STAT News · 2026-10-07 · ⏱ 5 min read

PhRMA has filed a lawsuit against the Trump administration's GLOBE pilot program, which ties Medicare reimbursement for certain drugs to international reference prices from peer countries — potentially reducing prices by 50-80% for affected drugs. The industry argues the program constitutes unlawful price controls dressed up as a voluntary model, while the administration contends it introduces market-based competition. The legal outcome will determine whether international reference pricing becomes a durable structural feature of Medicare drug payment.

💡 If the courts uphold the GLOBE model, it establishes a legal precedent enabling broad international reference pricing across Medicare, compressing margins for large-cap pharma and biopharma names with significant Medicare revenue exposure.
