🤖 Slot 3

OpenEvidence launches medical AI copilot feature that grades medical evidence and unveils NewYork-Presbyterian collaboration

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-07-09 · ⏱ 5 min read

OpenEvidence has launched EvidenceGrade, a feature that grades the quality of medical evidence behind every AI-generated clinical answer using an adaptation of the widely-adopted GRADE framework — scoring studies by design strength, consistency, precision, and clinical applicability in real time. The tool assigns letter grades (A through D) to help clinicians understand how much weight underlying evidence can bear, directly targeting the hallucination and source-conflation risks that have hampered clinical AI trust. The launch is paired with a system-wide deployment at NewYork-Presbyterian and its affiliated medical schools Columbia and Weill Cornell, adding to an existing Mount Sinai collaboration and a base of 690,000+ verified U.S. physicians.

💡 EvidenceGrade represents a meaningful productization moat in clinical AI — competing on epistemic transparency rather than raw accuracy — and the NewYork-Presbyterian anchor deal signals enterprise health system adoption is accelerating for purpose-built medical LLMs over general-purpose alternatives.
