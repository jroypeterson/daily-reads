AstraZeneca, Ionis data spark debate about RNA drugs' impact on deadly heart disease

BioPharma Dive · 2026-08-29 · ⏱ 5 min read

Full Phase 3 CARDIO-TTRansform data published in NEJM confirmed eplontersen (Wainua) missed its primary endpoint in ATTR-CM patients on standard of care, with event rates of 29% vs. 32% — a statistically non-significant difference. The failure raises questions about whether RNA 'silencers' like eplontersen and Alnylam's Amvuttra provide meaningful additive benefit on top of TTR 'stabilizers' (Vyndamax, Attruby), which now dominate standard of care. Analysts see limited direct commercial damage to Amvuttra but flag material risk to Alnylam's next-generation asset nucresiran, whose pivotal trial design may need to be restructured toward monotherapy.

💡 The trial failure reshapes the ATTR-CM competitive landscape and introduces a credible overhang on Alnylam's long-term pipeline thesis, making it actionable for investors holding or evaluating ALNY, AZN, IONS, or emerging TTR 'depleter' programs at AstraZeneca, Novo Nordisk, and Attralus.

🤖 Slot 3

[scuttleslops] AI Data Center Economics, ADI, INTU, IDXX

Scuttleblurb · 2026-08-29 · ⏱ 5 min read

A former AWS data center executive, via InPractise, breaks down the unit economics of hyperscaler AI infrastructure: a fully loaded 1-GW AI campus runs $40–45B (roughly $12–15B for physical infrastructure, $6–8B for power/transmission, and $25–30B for compute hardware), with current base-case IRRs of 15–20% that could reach 35–40% by 2028 if inference scales. He explains AWS's strategy of building ahead of demand to lock up scarce land and power, even at 30–40% power cost premiums. The key forward-looking insight is that Trainium 3 — not Trainium 2 — is expected to materially shift the return equation by pushing 3–4x more useful compute through each constrained megawatt.

💡 This ground-level cost curve analysis is directly actionable for sizing hyperscaler capex theses, evaluating power infrastructure investments, and understanding when AI infrastructure economics inflect — critical inputs for any investor with exposure to cloud, semiconductor, or data center names.
