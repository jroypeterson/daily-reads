🤖 Slot 3

Rethinking the Data Moat

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-20 · ⏱ 7 min read

MBI Deep Dives synthesizes arguments from Redwood Research's Ryan Greenblatt and Meta's Shuchao Bi to challenge the assumption that proprietary human expert data is the dominant driver of AI model improvement. Greenblatt argues that 'algorithmic progress' — better understanding of how to curate and filter data distributions — accounts for more model gains than the raw data itself, while Bi frames the issue as moving toward 'equalizing intelligence per token' rather than scaling raw data volume. Together, these perspectives suggest that data moats may be less durable than investors assume, as the compounding edge lies in the science of data processing rather than data ownership.

💡 For investors with positions in data-rich businesses (health IT, labs, diagnostics) where proprietary data is a core thesis, this piece raises a structurally important question about whether that moat is being hollowed out by algorithmic and synthetic data advances — directly actionable for portfolio thesis review.
