BioPharma Dive / STAT News · 2026-05-14 · ⏱ 3 min read

McKinsey explores space-based data centers as solution to AI infrastructure demands

McKinsey & Company · 2026-05-14 · ⏱ 6 min read

McKinsey projects a potential $1 trillion annual market for orbital data center build-out, driven by AI compute demands that could dominate global infrastructure needs. Space-based systems could reduce costs to under $5 million per megawatt versus $12-15 million for terrestrial data centers. Deutsche Bank expects first small-scale orbital data centers by 2027-2028, with larger constellations emerging in the 2030s.

💡 This represents a paradigm shift in data infrastructure strategy, offering investors and technologists a glimpse into how the AI compute bottleneck might be solved through space-based solutions with significant cost advantages and unlimited solar energy access.
