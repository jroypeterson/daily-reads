FDA OKs Takeda, Protagonist drug for rare blood cancer

BioPharma Dive · 2026-09-01 · ⏱ 3 min read

The FDA approved Mimrylo (rusfertide), a first-in-class hepcidin-mimetic developed by Protagonist Therapeutics and licensed to Takeda, for polycythemia vera — a rare blood cancer affecting ~150,000 U.S. patients. The label arrived ahead of its PDUFA date and without a mandatory prior-therapy requirement, prompting Jefferies to project $2B in peak annual sales. Protagonist receives $275M in approval-related payouts from Takeda and remains eligible for up to $875M more in milestones plus royalties.

💡 The broader-than-expected label removes a key adoption hurdle and could materially lift consensus revenue estimates for both PRTG (royalty/milestone beneficiary) and Takeda, while validating the hepcidin-mimetic mechanism for potential read-across to other rare hematology programs.
