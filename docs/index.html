Insurers See Positive Results in Managing Health Costs in Q2

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-08-09 · ⏱ 4 min read

Major managed care insurers posted a strong Q2 2026 rebound, with UnitedHealth earning $5.5B and CVS nearly $3B in quarterly profit, both beating analyst expectations after a difficult 2025. However, analysts at Leerink Partners caution that Medicaid margins remain under pressure—with MLRs near 94% at Centene—and upcoming state-level work requirement rollouts could create fresh headwinds in early 2027. Medicare Advantage shows structural improvement through repricing and footprint exits, but star ratings litigation is creating investor uncertainty around forward guidance.
