Moderna flu vaccine wins unanimous support from FDA panel

BioPharma Dive · 2026-06-19 · ⏱ 5 min read

The FDA's VRBPAC voted 9-0 on June 18 to endorse Moderna's mRNA-1010 (mFlusiva) for adults 50 and older, marking the first mRNA-based seasonal flu vaccine to clear this advisory hurdle after a dramatic regulatory reversal — the FDA had previously issued a Refusal to File letter in February 2026 under then-vaccine chief Vinay Prasad. The approval is based on a New England Journal of Medicine study showing 27% relative efficacy vs. standard-dose flu vaccine, with panelists acknowledging limitations (single flu season, limited influenza B data) but expressing confidence given a large confirmatory trial commitment of up to 800,000 participants. A final FDA decision is expected by August 5, potentially placing the vaccine on shelves for the 2026–2027 flu season.
