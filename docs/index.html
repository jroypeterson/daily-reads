MBI Deep Dives · 2026-10-24 · ⏱ 9 min read

Jefferson Health's ambient AI users closed charts up to 39% faster

Becker's Hospital Review · 2026-10-24 · ⏱ 3 min read · 🔒 snippet-only (paywalled, verified on summary)

A peer-reviewed study published in NEJM Catalyst examined Jefferson Health's ambient AI deployment across 5,234 clinicians, finding the most active users closed charts up to 39.3% faster and spent 16.6% less time on after-hours documentation. The scale and rigor of the dataset make this one of the strongest real-world validations of ambient AI productivity gains in healthcare to date. The findings support the commercial case for ambient AI platforms like Nuance/Microsoft DAX and emerging pure-plays in the space.

💡 Hard outcome data from a large-scale, peer-reviewed deployment significantly de-risks the ambient AI investment thesis and provides diligenceable evidence for evaluating Nuance/Microsoft and ambient AI competitors.
