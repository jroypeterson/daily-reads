'Unlikely' AstraZeneca-BMS merger could reset deal landscape

BioPharma Dive · 2026-08-04 · ⏱ 4 min read

Reports of exploratory merger talks between AstraZeneca and Bristol Myers Squibb sent both stocks lower, with AstraZeneca shedding ~$22B in market cap as Wall Street analysts questioned the strategic rationale given AZN's strong organic growth trajectory. Key concerns include antitrust exposure (especially in the UK), patent cliff pressures on BMY's top products, and difficulty reaching price alignment. Beyond the deal itself, analysts note the rumor signals a potential shift back toward transformational megamergers after years of bolt-on biotech acquisitions dominating big pharma M&A.

💡 Whether or not the deal closes, the AZN-BMY rumor is recalibrating expectations around large-cap pharma M&A strategy — potentially pulling two major acquirers off the market while signaling that transformational deals may re-enter the playbook, with direct implications for pipeline and valuation dynamics across oncology and cardiovascular names.

Letter #339: Oscar Hattink (2026)

A Letter a Day · 2026-08-04 · ⏱ 6 min read

The letter traces BlueDrive's arc from $10M in 2017 to $500M AUM in 2021, through the meme-stock collapse that cost them 19% while the MSCI World rose 20%, and into a multi-year underperformance driven by a heavy cash position and tail-hedge strategy as AI-driven momentum overwhelmed their value discipline. Hattink reflects honestly on the difference between knowing investment principles intellectually and having the wisdom to apply them under pressure — and chooses integrity over strategy-drift.

💡 For a reader drawn to competitive moat analysis, corporate governance, and deep business diligence, this letter offers a rare first-person account of how a disciplined concentrated manager navigates — and ultimately loses to — irrational markets, surfacing timeless lessons about when conviction becomes stubbornness.
