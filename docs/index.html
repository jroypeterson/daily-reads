Nvidia Climbing the Wall of Worries

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-27 · ⏱ 7 min read

MBI Deep Dives unpacks Nvidia's latest earnings, noting that FY2028 sell-side revenue estimates were off by ~$400B just a year ago, and that management's ~70% guided growth may represent a floor given supply constraints. The piece dissects a key tension: Nvidia is actively cultivating neoclouds to commoditize hyperscalers, while hyperscalers are simultaneously the primary customers filling neocloud backlogs — a structural risk if hyperscalers close the capacity gap. Despite staggering operating outperformance, NVDA has barely outperformed the S&P 500, reflecting a buy-side skepticism that the stock must 'climb the wall of worries' to re-rate.

💡 For investors, the article reframes NVDA not as a momentum trade but as a structural thesis stress-test — the neocloud dependency, frontier lab concentration risk, and margin sustainability questions are the real valuation variables that will determine whether the stock catches up to its fundamentals.
