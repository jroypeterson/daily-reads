📊 Slot 2

Medicare will cut billions from 340B, expand site-neutral payments in 2027

BioPharma Dive · 2026-11-06 · ⏱ 4 min read

CMS finalized its 2027 hospital outpatient prospective payment rule, cutting reimbursement rates for drugs acquired under the 340B discount program and expanding site-neutral payment policies that equalize rates between hospital outpatient departments and physician offices. Hospitals fiercely opposed the rule, arguing it undermines their ability to cross-subsidize care for underserved populations. Together, the 340B cuts and site-neutral expansion represent a meaningful, multi-billion-dollar structural headwind to hospital system revenues beginning in 2027.

💡 This finalized rule is an actionable reimbursement catalyst — directly pressuring margins for hospital operators (HCA, THC, UHS) and creating downstream effects on specialty pharma and drug distributors that rely on 340B economics, while potentially benefiting managed care payers through lower site-of-care costs.
