Alphabet's Isomorphic Labs raises $2.1 billion Series B for AI drug discovery revolution

Multiple Tech Sources · 2026-05-15 · ⏱ 3 min read

🌀 Slot 4

DOJ accuses Yale and UCLA medical schools of racial discrimination in admissions process

STAT News · 2026-05-15 · ⏱ 6 min read

The DOJ has accused Yale and UCLA medical schools of racially discriminatory admissions practices, citing MCAT score disparities between racial groups as evidence of continued affirmative action despite the 2023 Supreme Court ban. The investigation is part of a broader Trump administration campaign against DEI programs, creating legal uncertainty for medical schools who face potential violations whether they explicitly consider race or adopt race-neutral policies with diverse outcomes. Medical educators argue that MCAT scores are imperfect measures of physician potential and that broadened admission criteria better serve healthcare needs.

💡 This legal precedent could fundamentally reshape medical education pipelines and healthcare workforce composition, with downstream effects on health outcomes, medical school operations, and the broader healthcare delivery system.
