Midjourney Medical: AI Image Company Unveils Full-Body Ultrasonic Scanner

Midjourney (via Hacker News) · 2026-06-18 · ⏱ 5 min read

Midjourney has announced Midjourney Medical, a new division launching a full-body ultrasonic CT scanner using ~500,000 transducers to generate MRI-grade whole-body imaging in ~60 seconds with no radiation, processing roughly 17GB/s of data across a large compute cluster. The hardware is built on an exclusive technology license from Butterfly Network (BFLY) signed in late 2025, with the first consumer 'Midjourney Spa' planned for San Francisco in late 2027. The company has stated ambitious targets of 50,000 scanners and 1 billion scans per month by 2031, framing the product as a casual, consumer wellness experience rather than a clinical device.

💡 Midjourney's entry into medical imaging at consumer scale — backed by a BFLY exclusive license — is a direct competitive and M&A signal for diagnostic imaging incumbents (GE HealthCare, Siemens Healthineers) and raises immediate questions about BFLY's strategic positioning, while the mass-scale body data infrastructure play is highly relevant to digital health and AI-in-biology investors.

Health Services Deal Value Holds Steady in 2026 With Higher Bar for Investment: PwC

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-06-18 · ⏱ 5 min read

PwC's midyear 2026 health services M&A outlook finds deal volume softening (300 deals in Q1 2026, down from 324 in Q3 2025) while total deal value held resilient at $18B in Q1, driven by larger, conviction-backed transactions. Physician medical groups captured a record 46% of Q1 deal volume with 18% year-over-year growth, while eHealth captured 61% of disclosed deal value despite a 29% drop in deal count. Strategic acquirers and PE sponsors are shifting their primary question from growth to earnings sustainability and reimbursement predictability, with policy uncertainty around Medicare Advantage and government-sponsored populations serving as the key overhang.

💡 PwC's granular subsector data and the 'repricing of risk' framework give investors a practical lens for identifying which healthcare assets (reimbursement-stable, cash-generative platforms in dentistry, oncology, ophthalmology, behavioral health) are most likely to attract premium M&A multiples in H2 2026.
