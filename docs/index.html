BREAKING: mRNA Cancer Vaccine Succeeds in Late-Stage Trial

STAT News · 2026-08-22 · ⏱ 5 min read

Moderna shares surged ~177% on the news while Merck rose ~12%, with Leerink analysts projecting ~$1.4B in average annual revenue by 2032. The result accelerates a broader platform thesis, with major trials now underway in lung, bladder, kidney, and pancreatic cancers.

Rethinking the Data Moat

MBI Deep Dives (Substack) · 2026-08-22 · ⏱ 7 min read

MBI Deep Dives interrogates the assumption that proprietary human expert data is the primary driver of AI model improvements, drawing on Ryan Greenblatt (Redwood Research) and Shuchao Bi (Meta Superintelligence Labs) to argue that algorithmic curation and data processing science matter more than raw data ownership. Greenblatt's thought experiment — that GPT-3-level compute trained today would yield a model better than GPT-4 — illustrates how much improvement comes from algorithmic progress rather than data accumulation. The author concludes that data moats may be weaker than investors assume, urging epistemic humility about AI landscape views.

💡 Directly challenges the valuation logic used to justify data-moat premiums in health data platforms, diagnostics companies, and AI-native healthcare businesses — with implications for how investors should underwrite proprietary dataset advantages.
