Sanofi's $41B Insmed buy biggest since Pfizer/Seagen

BioPharma Dive · 2026-11-24 · ⏱ 4 min read

Sanofi is acquiring Insmed for approximately $41 billion, marking the largest biopharma deal since Pfizer's acquisition of Seagen. The deal gives Sanofi access to Insmed's two commercial products — including treprostinil for nontuberculous mycobacterial lung disease (NTMBC) — and a broader pipeline in immunology and rare disease, areas Sanofi is prioritizing as it looks to diversify away from its legacy portfolio. Sanofi characterized the deal as 'transformative,' signaling continued big-pharma appetite for durable rare-disease franchises with strong pricing power.

💡 This landmark deal reshapes the rare-disease competitive landscape and sets a valuation benchmark for similar pipeline assets, with immediate implications for INSM shareholders and sector positioning in immunology and orphan drug M&A.
