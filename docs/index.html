Humana rebounds, Aetna slips: Winners and losers in the 2027 MA star ratings

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-10-10 · ⏱ 5 min read

CMS released 2027 Medicare Advantage star ratings, with Humana emerging as the dominant winner — 93% of its members are now in 4-star-or-higher plans, up from just 20% in 2026, restoring eligibility for quality bonus payments and triggering a significant share price reaction. CVS Health's Aetna is the notable loser, with 4-star member share falling to 69% from 81%, as three of its four largest contracts declined. UnitedHealthcare also saw modest deterioration (67% from 78%), while Clover Health reversed dramatically from 3% to 100% in 4-star plans amid ongoing litigation that could prompt further legal challenges from disadvantaged insurers.

💡 Star ratings directly determine eligibility for billions in CMS quality bonus payments, making this a high-impact catalyst for managed care earnings in 2027-2028 and reshaping competitive positioning across the Medicare Advantage sector heading into October open enrollment.

Room at the Top (MBI Deep Dives)

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-10-10 · ⏱ 6 min read

MBI Deep Dives dissects the extraordinary concentration of the S&P 500, showing that the bottom 434 companies by market cap equal the top 5 — a level exceeding the dot-com peak — and argues this isn't mere valuation distortion since earnings and capex are equally concentrated (hyperscaler LTM capex of ~$586B matches the bottom ~420 firms combined). The piece explores the churn dynamic within the concentrated group, noting nearly half of the top 15 S&P 500 companies in 2026 weren't on the list in 2022, driven by AI value chain winners displacing non-participants. It closes by asking whether Schumpeterian creative destruction will continue to refresh the top or whether political backlash from entrenched concentration becomes a structural risk.

💡 Practitioner-grade structural analysis of index concentration and AI capex dynamics that directly informs portfolio construction decisions — particularly factor exposure to mega-cap vs. the rest, and whether today's capex buildout translates into tomorrow's earnings moats or regulatory headwinds.
