Capricor Duchenne cell therapy voted down by FDA panel

BioPharma Dive · 2026-07-30 · ⏱ 4 min read

An FDA advisory panel voted 9-3 against Capricor's cell therapy deramiocel for Duchenne-related cardiomyopathy, finding the evidence insufficient due to disputed statistical analysis changes that reviewers called scientifically unsupported. The vote is a significant barometer given the FDA's recent trend of reviving previously rejected drugs under new leadership, and the agency must issue a final decision by August 22. Notably, a same-day Lancet publication revealed deramiocel missed statistical significance on key cardiac endpoints, prompting Capricor to issue a regulatory clarification mid-meeting.

💡 With the FDA decision due August 22, this panel outcome and its underlying statistical controversy set a meaningful precedent for how the agency will weigh fragile evidence in rare pediatric indications — directly relevant to any biotech holding with orphan drug or accelerated approval exposure.
