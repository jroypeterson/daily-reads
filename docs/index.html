Novartis bets up to $7.8B for mRNA T cell engager from China's Abogen

BioSpace / Fierce Biotech · 2026-10-02 · ⏱ 3 min read

Novartis is acquiring global rights to Abogen's ABO2203 — an mRNA-encoded CD19xCD3 T cell engager delivered via lipid nanoparticles — for $575M upfront and up to $7.2B in milestones, plus options on Abogen's broader RNA platform. The deal follows Novartis pausing a rival autoimmune CAR-T program after three patient deaths, signaling a strategic shift toward in vivo mRNA-encoded immunology. Early Phase 1 data in ITP patients showed complete B cell depletion and platelet responses without cytokine release syndrome, supporting the modality's tolerability advantage over conventional cell therapies.

💡 This one of the largest China-to-West biopharma licensing deals of 2026 validates mRNA-encoded T cell engagers as a credible next frontier in autoimmune disease, with direct implications for Novartis's immunology pipeline strategy and broader mRNA platform valuations.

🤖 Slot 3

Cloudflare Clef: Open-Weight Decision Models and New RL Fine-Tuning Platform

Cloudflare Blog / Hacker News (ac25ef97) · 2026-10-02 · ⏱ 7 min read

Cloudflare has released Clef and Clef-flash, open-weight decision models hosted on Workers AI, designed to handle fast, structured classification tasks in agentic workflows — routing, escalation, content moderation — where general-purpose LLMs are too slow and expensive. Clef (27B parameters) achieves 209ms median latency versus 524ms for the leading competitor Jev, while Clef-flash (9B) runs under 40ms; both support vision encoding and a 64k context window. Cloudflare is simultaneously launching a reinforcement learning fine-tuning service so developers can adapt Clef to proprietary classification use cases on private data.

💡 Decision models are the critical routing and gating layer of production agentic AI systems, and Cloudflare's open-source release with edge-hosted inference represents a credible infrastructure moat play for NET at exactly the moment enterprise agentic adoption is accelerating — directly relevant to data infrastructure and AI impact themes the reader prioritizes.
