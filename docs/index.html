HCA Healthcare's H1 ACA Volume Dropoffs 'Almost One for One' Shifted to Uninsurance

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-07-25 · ⏱ 4 min read

HCA Healthcare's Q2 2026 results reveal that patients dropping ACA marketplace coverage due to higher premiums converted to uninsured status 'almost one for one' rather than shifting to commercial coverage — a worse outcome than management's 80-85% assumption. The payer mix deterioration delivered a ~$400M pre-tax EBITDA headwind in Q2 alone, pushing the full-year ACA subsidy impact to $1.0–$1.2B and forcing downward guidance revisions across net income, EBITDA, and EPS. Despite this, HCA still grew revenue 8.7% YoY and EPS 11.6% aided by share buybacks and solid insured volume trends outside exchanges.

💡 The one-for-one exchange-to-uninsured conversion pattern is a systemic signal for the entire for-profit hospital sector and has direct read-through to managed care payers, health IT spending, and the broader investment thesis around ACA subsidy expiration risk.
