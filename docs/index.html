HCA now expects up to $1.2B hit from ACA headwinds

Medicare Advantage insurers face new bipartisan scrutiny over AI and care denials

STAT News · 2026-07-15 · ⏱ 5 min read

Senators Blumenthal and Hawley have launched a rare bipartisan Senate probe into UnitedHealth Group, Humana, and CVS Health, demanding documents on their use of AI algorithms to deny or delay Medicare Advantage rehabilitative care. The inquiry follows a wave of class-action litigation alleging that tools like UnitedHealth's nH Predict systematically override physician recommendations, and arrives days after Optum disclosed its Anthropic AI partnership — heightening scrutiny of how AI is being operationalized in prior authorization. The congressional pressure adds a new regulatory and litigation risk layer to managed care operators already navigating elevated medical loss ratios and political headwinds on MA reimbursement.

💡 Bipartisan congressional scrutiny creates compounding governance, litigation, and regulatory risk for UNH, HUM, and CVS at a structurally sensitive moment for Medicare Advantage profitability, making this directly relevant for managed care positioning.
