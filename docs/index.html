Argenx sees unusual setback with failure of late-stage study of drug for autoimmune disease

STAT News · 2026-10-08 · ⏱ 4 min read

Argenx reported that Vyvgart (efgartigimod) failed to beat placebo in a Phase 3 trial for Sjögren's disease, an unexpected and significant miss given the drug's prior commercial success across other autoimmune indications. The failure is described as unusual for a drug with Vyvgart's track record and removes a meaningful TAM expansion opportunity that Wall Street had been pricing in. The setback compounds earlier pipeline disappointments and raises questions about the limits of FcRn inhibition as a platform across autoimmune diseases.

💡 The Phase 3 failure narrows Vyvgart's peak sales ceiling and is an actionable negative catalyst for ARGX longs, while also serving as a competitive read-through for other FcRn inhibitor developers such as Johnson & Johnson and UCB.
