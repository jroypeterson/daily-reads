CMS targets 340B, site-neutral rates in 2027 outpatient rule — 8 things to know

Becker's Payer Issues · 2026-07-03 · ⏱ 4 min read

CMS's proposed 2027 outpatient prospective payment rule targets two major revenue streams simultaneously: reduced 340B drug reimbursement rates and an expanded site-neutral payment policy covering new categories of outpatient services. The combined effect would strip billions from hospital outpatient revenue starting in 2027, with the eight-point breakdown detailing affected service categories, policy mechanics, and the regulatory rationale driving each change.
