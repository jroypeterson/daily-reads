BioPharma Dive: Moderna, Merck shares fall on eagerly anticipated cancer vaccine results

BioPharma Dive · 2026-10-23 · ⏱ 5 min read

The data triggered a sharp sell-the-news reaction (MRNA -25%, MRK -1%), with analyst opinion split between those calling it practice-changing and those flagging the slight efficacy deterioration as a commercial uncertainty.
