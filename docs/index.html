NCQA to use Komodo data, AI tool to develop future HEDIS measures

Fierce Healthcare · 2026-08-30 · ⏱ 3 min read

NCQA has entered a multi-year strategic partnership with Komodo Health to modernize HEDIS quality measure development using Komodo's Healthcare Map (330M+ de-identified patient journeys) and its Marmot AI platform. The partnership allows NCQA to evaluate measures across Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial populations from a single longitudinal data source, cutting testing time roughly in half — from two to three months to approximately one month, as demonstrated in the already-completed COF-E colorectal cancer screening measure. Komodo is now positioned as a key platform supporting HEDIS measure testing broadly across NCQA's portfolio going forward.

💡 Embedding Komodo's proprietary data infrastructure into the regulatory standard-setting backbone of managed care quality measurement represents a durable, compounding moat — with downstream implications for payers, providers, and competing health analytics platforms as HEDIS compliance shapes reimbursement and performance benchmarking across the industry.
