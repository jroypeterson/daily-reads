Pharma's $500B Patent Problem Will Come in Waves — Not as a Cliff

BioPharma Dive · 2026-10-12 · ⏱ 4 min read

Merck faces the most acute near-term pressure via Keytruda's 2028 expiry ($99B at risk that year alone), while AbbVie's challenge intensifies in 2032 as Rinvoq and Skyrizi lose protection.
