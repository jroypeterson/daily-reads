AI is fabricating citations in medical journals

STAT News · 2026-05-08 · ⏱ 5 min read

A Columbia University study analyzing 97 million citations found AI-fabricated references spreading rapidly in biomedical literature, rising from 1 in 2,828 papers in 2023 to 1 in 277 in early 2026. The researchers identified 4,000 fabricated citations across 2,800 papers, with over a third concentrated in two large open-access publishers. The trend reflects deeper problems with research culture as AI tools enable superficial engagement with literature.

💡 This represents a fundamental threat to evidence-based medicine and could undermine the scientific foundation that healthcare investments, drug approvals, and clinical guidelines depend upon.

Angelini to buy Catalyst in $4B play for rare neuro drugs

BioPharma Dive · 2026-05-08 · ⏱ 3 min read

Italian pharma Angelini will acquire Catalyst Pharmaceuticals for $4.1 billion ($31.50/share, 21% premium), gaining three approved CNS treatments including Firdapse and Fycompa that generated $589M revenue in 2025. The deal gives Angelini its first major U.S. market entry and reflects broader pharma consolidation in neuroscience, joining recent billion-dollar CNS acquisitions by UCB, Otsuka, and Eli Lilly.

💡 Large-scale M&A demonstrates continued appetite for revenue-generating rare disease assets and highlights strategic consolidation in neuroscience as a key 2026 trend worth monitoring for investment implications.
