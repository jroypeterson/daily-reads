🤖 Slot 3

Rethinking the Data Moat

MBI Deep Dives · 2026-08-24 · ⏱ 7 min read

MBI Deep Dives synthesizes recent arguments from Redwood Research's Ryan Greenblatt and former OpenAI/Meta researcher Shuchao Bi to challenge the idea that proprietary data is the primary driver of AI progress. Both researchers argue that algorithmic improvements — better data curation, filtering pipelines, and RL environment design — matter more than raw data accumulation or human expert data. This reframes the competitive moat question: the durable advantage may lie in the *process* of data refinement rather than data ownership itself.

💡 For investors evaluating software and platform businesses on data moat durability, this piece provides a well-reasoned framework for distinguishing genuinely defensible data advantages from those that will erode as algorithmic curation and AI labor commoditize raw data collection.
